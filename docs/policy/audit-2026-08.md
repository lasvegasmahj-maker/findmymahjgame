# Policy and Legal Product Readiness Audit, August 2026

> SUPERSEDED IN PART, 2026-08-23: the owner-approved provider business model resolved the
> free-period conflict flagged below. The 6-month FINDMYMAHJGAME code and the 12-month Stripe
> coupon path (allow_promotion_codes) are both retired in code; the complimentary period is now
> a 90-day app-managed Premium trial starting at claim, no card, no auto-billing. Findings about
> those two mechanisms are historical record, not open items. See
> docs/provider-business-model-proposed-decision.md (APPROVED).

This is an engineering and content audit, not legal advice. It compares every policy surface
on findmymahjgame.com against the product as it actually exists in code today. Every claim
below cites a file. Where a fact came from project memory instead of code (the billing grace
model), that is called out explicitly as not yet built.

Scope: Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Advertiser Terms, provider/listing terms, matching terms
and community standards, billing and subscription disclosure, age policy, communications
consent, and data deletion. Drafts for owner review live in `docs/policy/drafts/`. The launch
gate checklist lives in `docs/policy/launch-gate.md`.

## 1. Terms of Use

**Live at:** `/terms`, `app/terms/page.tsx`. Last updated label says "April 2026."

**Current claim:** A directory-only platform. Player listings are free, advertiser listings are
paid, no verification of any user, in-person meetings are entirely at the user's own risk, no
mention of accounts.

**Current reality:**
- Passwordless accounts exist (`lib/user-auth.ts` session model; `app/api/auth/signin/route.ts`;
  `app/account/account-client.tsx`). The Terms never mention an account, a session, or what
  signing in means.
- Provider claims are now an account-linked, evidence-based process
  (`supabase/migrations/2026-08-24-wave2-foundations.sql` lines 6 to 42: `evidence`,
  `confidence`, `decided_by`, `decided_at` added to `listing_claims`). Terms still describe only
  the free-text "Player Listings" and "Advertiser Listings" model from before accounts existed.
- Mahj Match (18+ matchmaking) does not appear anywhere in this document, even though it is
  built (`lib/match/consent.ts`, `lib/match/states.ts`, `app/api/cron/matcher/route.ts`).
- Blocking and reporting tables exist (`user_blocks`, `user_reports` in the same migration,
  lines 71 to 97) but the Terms' only safety language is the generic "report a user by email"
  line already there. There is no mention of an in-product block or report action.
- The "In-Person Meetings" and duplicate "Meeting Other Players" sections are reasonable content
  but were clearly written before Mahj Match existed; they read as though every connection is
  self-initiated off-platform, when the product now proposes tables algorithmically.

**Verdict: Stale.** It is not wrong about what it covers, but it covers roughly half of what the
product now does. See `docs/policy/drafts/terms-of-use.md` and the new
`docs/policy/drafts/provider-terms.md` and `docs/policy/drafts/matching-community-standards.md`,
which this draft now points to instead of trying to fold everything into one document.

## 2. Privacy Policy

**Live at:** `/privacy`, `app/privacy/page.tsx`. Last updated label says "May 2026." `robots:
{index:false, follow:false}`.

**Current claim:** "When you create a listing or contact us, we collect only what you provide:
your name, city, email address, and any details you include in your listing." Cookies are
described as function-only, with "privacy-respecting analytics tools" in vague terms. Removal
requests are processed "within 7 days."

**Current reality, what is actually collected:**
- Account: email (lowercased), display name, city, state
  (`docs/membership-system-spec.md` section A `profiles` table; confirmed live in
  `app/api/account/route.ts`, which reads and writes `display_name` and `deactivated_at`).
- Listings: city, skill level, availability, a 200-character bio, contact email
  (`app/list-my-game/list-my-game-client.tsx` lines 37 to 64). The public name is truncated
  server-side to "First L." format by `enforcePublicName()` (`lib/sanitize.ts` lines 23 to 35),
  never the full legal name.
- Matching: adult affirmation timestamp, consent version, opt-in timestamp, city, state, travel
  radius (1 to 200 miles), availability, mahjong variant, skill, social style, hosting
  preference, and group preference
  (`supabase/migrations/2026-08-24-wave2-foundations.sql` lines 48 to 67, table
  `matching_profiles`). None of this is mentioned in the Privacy Policy at all.
- First-party analytics: a fixed list of event names (signup, listing, search, ask, matching,
  billing events) with scrubbed properties, a session key, and a record class
  (`lib/analytics/events.ts`; table `analytics_events` in the same migration, lines 126 to 136).
  The scrubber (`scrubProps`, lines 56 to 65) strips any property key matching
  `q|question|text|query|email|name|address|phone|bio|detail|message` and caps string values at
  64 characters, so free text and direct identifiers never reach the analytics table. This is a
  real, verifiable privacy protection that the current policy gets no credit for because it is
  not disclosed.
- A notification ledger recording every transactional email sent: kind, subject, delivery
  status, and which record it relates to, but not the email body
  (`lib/notifications/notify.ts`; table `notifications_log`, migration lines 103 to 119).
- Blocking and reporting records when a user blocks or reports another user
  (`user_blocks`, `user_reports`, migration lines 71 to 97).

**Current reality, what is never collected or shown:**
- Exact street addresses. `lib/private-location.ts` actively detects and blocks street-level
  detail (house numbers, cross streets) and private-residence language in venue, address,
  description, and day/time fields, and can redact it (`redactStreetDetail()`, lines 86 to 99).
  The safe public fallback is city and state only, with a note that "the host shares the
  location once you join the group" (`safePublicVenue()`, lines 73 to 76).
- Email addresses are never shown publicly. Confirmed in product copy
  (`app/list-my-game/list-my-game-client.tsx` line 155) and in the FAQ
  (`app/faq/page.tsx` line 40).
- In matching, nothing about another player is shown beyond first name and shared compatibility
  facts until both sides accept a seat (`lib/match/states.ts` lines 17 to 19, comment on
  `SEAT_STATUSES`).
- Card numbers. Billing tables store only Stripe customer and subscription IDs, status, and
  price ID, never card data; the schema comment states Stripe is the sole source of financial
  truth (`supabase/migrations/2026-08-23-billing.sql`, table comments throughout).

**Overclaim found:** "We will process all removal requests within 7 days" (`app/privacy/page.tsx`
line 58) is an operational promise with no code enforcement anywhere (no queue, no SLA timer, no
ticketing found in the repo). It may be true as a matter of practice, but nothing in the code
guarantees it, so this line should stay only if the owner is confident it is being met in
practice.

**Verdict: Stale, with one overclaim.** The privacy policy describes roughly the 2026 April
version of the site. See `docs/policy/drafts/privacy-policy.md`.

## 3. Advertiser Terms

**Live at:** `/advertiser-terms`, `app/advertiser-terms/page.tsx`. Last updated "April 2026."

**Current claim:** Monthly and annual advertiser plans, paid on invoice, cancellable with 30 days
notice, with a described refund schedule.

**Current reality:** The live pricing on `/join` (`app/join/page.tsx`) is a single $89/year
Directory Membership, with a promo code (FINDMYMAHJGAME) that currently makes the first 6 months
free and is handled manually, no card collected today (`app/get-listed/get-listed-client.tsx`
line 75 comment: "billing is set up manually until the Stripe build lands"). There is also a
separate advertiser/sponsor promo code, FINDMYMAHJ, offering 6 months free plus 30% off for
life, used on the `/advertise` inquiry flow (`app/api/advertise-inquiry/route.ts` lines 27 and
109). Advertiser Terms describes neither of these live mechanics; it describes a pricing
structure that predates both.

**Verdict: Stale.** This page should either be rewritten to match the current $89/year model
described in `docs/policy/drafts/billing-disclosures.md`, or clearly scoped to cover only the
separate sponsor/advertiser track (`/advertise`, code FINDMYMAHJ) if that remains a distinct
paid-placement product from the Directory Membership. That distinction is an owner decision; see
the `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]` list.

## 4. Provider/listing terms

**Live at:** nowhere separate. Folded loosely into `/terms`.

**Current reality:** Provider claims are now a real, evidence-based, account-linked flow:
`listing_claims` gained `profile_id`, `evidence` (jsonb), `confidence` (high, medium, low),
`decision_reason`, `decided_by` (system or admin), and `decided_at`
(`supabase/migrations/2026-08-24-wave2-foundations.sql` lines 6 to 27). A claim can only be
decided by the system or an admin, never assumed. There is no public document explaining to a
teacher or organizer what claiming a listing means, what evidence is reviewed, or what happens
if a claim is rejected or needs more information (the notification kinds `claim_received`,
`claim_approved`, `claim_needs_info`, `claim_rejected` all exist in
`lib/notifications/notify.ts` lines 17 to 20 but have no public explanation).

**Verdict: Missing entirely.** See `docs/policy/drafts/provider-terms.md`.

## 5. Matching terms and community standards

**Live at:** nowhere. No page, no footer link, no mention.

**Current reality:** Mahj Match is a real, built system.
- Consent is a database fact, versioned (`CONSENT_VERSION = "2026-08-v1"`,
  `lib/match/consent.ts` line 6), requiring an active account, an explicit adult affirmation, an
  explicit opt-in at the current consent version, and no deactivation, before any matching
  operation runs (`readMatchingConsent()`, lines 18 to 39).
- The table lifecycle only reveals a player's first name and shared compatibility facts once
  both sides accept a proposed seat (`lib/match/states.ts` lines 10 to 21).
- Blocking (`user_blocks`) and reporting (`user_reports`, with categories harassment,
  spam/scam, unsafe, false identity, other, and a status workflow that includes
  `needs_human`) exist at the schema level
  (`supabase/migrations/2026-08-24-wave2-foundations.sql` lines 71 to 97).

**Important gap found during this audit:** `readMatchingConsent()` is defined but has zero call
sites anywhere else in the codebase. The currently live match-approval route
(`app/api/match/decide/route.ts`) and the matcher cron (`app/api/cron/matcher/route.ts`) do not
call it. So the 18+ consent gate is a correct, well-designed contract, but it is not yet proven
to run on every matching path. The `playerMatching` launch gate should not flip, and no public
"we require 18+ and explicit consent" claim should go live, until every entry point into
matching calls `readMatchingConsent()` and fails closed when it returns ineligible. This is
flagged again in the launch gate document.

**Also found:** there is no player-facing UI for matching at all yet (no consent screen, no
opt-in form, no "18+" language anywhere in `app/`, confirmed by search). The only matching page
that exists, `app/match/confirm/page.tsx`, is an admin approval screen reached from an emailed
link, not a player-facing flow.

**Verdict: Missing entirely**, and the backend enforcement needs to be finished and verified
before this draft can be published as a true statement of how the product behaves. See
`docs/policy/drafts/matching-community-standards.md`.

## 6. Billing and subscription disclosure

**Live at:** nowhere as a legal disclosure. Pricing appears as marketing copy on `/join`
(`app/join/page.tsx`), `/get-listed` (`app/get-listed/get-listed-client.tsx`), the homepage FAQ
schema (`lib/schema.ts` line 375), and `/how-it-works`
(`app/how-it-works/how-it-works-client.tsx` line 106).

**A real inconsistency found in the code, not just missing disclosure:** two different free-period
mechanisms exist and they do not agree with each other.

1. **The live, manual mechanism** (what actually runs today): a teacher or organizer submits
   `/get-listed` with promo code FINDMYMAHJGAME. The code comment is explicit: "Founding code is
   accepted directly; billing is set up manually until the Stripe build lands"
   (`app/get-listed/get-listed-client.tsx` line 73 to 75). No card is collected. The confirmation
   email says: "As a Charter Member, your first 6 months are free. Nothing is charged today, we
   will email you to set up billing before the free period ends" (`app/api/get-listed/route.ts`
   line 178). This matches the "no card at launch" model.

2. **The dark, automated mechanism** (built, gated behind `launch_payments`, not live): Stripe
   Checkout for a single **annual** price of $89 (`STRIPE_PRICE_MEMBERSHIP_ANNUAL`,
   `app/api/billing/checkout/route.ts` line 22), with promotion codes enabled
   (`allow_promotion_codes: true`, same file). The runbook instructs creating a coupon that is
   "100 percent off... Duration: Once (this makes the first billing period free; regular $89
   billing starts automatically after that)" (`docs/billing-launch-runbook.md` lines 32 to 35).
   Because the price itself is annual, a "duration once" coupon on it waives an entire **year**,
   not 6 months, and Stripe Checkout in subscription mode collects a payment method at checkout
   time, which contradicts "nothing is charged today."

These two mechanisms describe different offers (6 months manually deferred, versus 12 months
automated with a card collected upfront) and nothing in the code reconciles what happens to a
Charter Member who joined manually once the automated Stripe path goes live. This needs an
owner decision before any billing disclosure can be published truthfully. It is listed in the
`[OWNER TO CONFIRM]` section below and in the launch gate document.

**Payment failure handling:** the webhook sets a subscription's status to `past_due` on
`invoice.payment_failed` (`app/api/billing/webhook/route.ts` lines 147 to 163) and nothing else.
No dunning email is sent automatically; the notification kind `membership_billing_status`
exists in the taxonomy (`lib/notifications/notify.ts` line 22) but `notify()` has zero call
sites anywhere in the app. The 14-day grace period, dunning sequence, and automatic revert to a
free basic listing described in project memory as the intended policy is **not yet built**. The
billing disclosure draft describes this intended policy per the mission brief, but the audit
must be explicit: as of this commit, nothing in the code implements a grace period, a dunning
email, or an automatic downgrade. Publishing the grace-period promise without building it first
is a real risk.

**Card storage:** confirmed nowhere are card numbers stored. `billing_customers`,
`billing_subscriptions`, and `billing_events` hold only Stripe IDs, status, and price
references; the migration's own comments state Stripe is the sole source of financial truth and
these tables are never proof of payment (`supabase/migrations/2026-08-23-billing.sql`).

**Verdict: Missing, and the underlying mechanism needs an owner decision before it can be
described accurately.** See `docs/policy/drafts/billing-disclosures.md`.

## 7. Age policy

**Live at:** nowhere. No age gate, no age statement, anywhere in `app/`. Confirmed by searching
the entire `app/` tree for "18+", "adult", and "minor": zero matches outside the backend
consent library.

**Current reality:** the site itself is general audience (player listings, teacher listings,
events, all viewable and creatable with no age check). Only Mahj Match is 18+, enforced
exclusively in the database via `matching_profiles.adult_affirmed_at` and checked by
`readMatchingConsent()` (`lib/match/consent.ts`). As noted in section 5, that check is not yet
wired into the live matching routes.

**Verdict: Missing.** A general audience statement plus an explicit 18+ notice scoped to
matching only belongs in Terms of Use and in the new matching community standards document. See
both drafts.

## 8. Communications consent

**Live at:** `/unsubscribe` (`app/unsubscribe/unsubscribe-client.tsx`) for Mailchimp marketing
mail, `/newsletter` for signup. This works and is accurately described: single opt-in, no
confirmation email, per project memory, and the unsubscribe flow immediately and permanently
stops mail to that address (`app/unsubscribe/unsubscribe-client.tsx` line 27 and 35).

**Current reality on the transactional side:** `lib/notifications/notify.ts` defines a full
taxonomy of transactional messages (account, provider, and matching notifications) that are, by
the code's own comment, "the product cannot operate without" and are kept separate from
Mailchimp marketing on purpose (same file, lines 6 to 9). This is the right design. But:
- Nowhere on the site is this distinction (transactional versus marketing) disclosed to a user.
- `notify()` itself has zero call sites anywhere in the app right now. Every one of the 16
  notification kinds is defined but dark. This is consistent with the Wave 2 "ships dark"
  pattern described in the migration file's header comment, but it means no transactional email
  the drafts describe is actually being sent today.

**Verdict: Accurate as far as it goes (marketing), incomplete (transactional is undisclosed and
unwired).** The privacy policy draft adds a plain-language transactional-versus-marketing
section.

## 9. Data deletion

**Live at:** `app/api/account/route.ts`, action `request_deletion` (lines 64 to 67).

**Current reality:** matches the mission brief closely. The code comment states the design
intent directly: "Deletion is a logged request handled by a person, not an instant destructive
write, so a hijacked session cannot erase someone's history before they notice" (lines 9 to 11).
The action inserts a row into `account_events` with `deletion_requested` and returns the message
"We received your deletion request and will confirm by email once it is done."

**Overclaim found:** that response promises an email confirmation, but the code does not call
`notify()` or `sendEmail()` anywhere in this handler. No automated email is sent. A human has to
remember to email the requester back manually once the deletion is actually done. The claim of
being logged and reviewed by a person is accurate; the claim of "we will confirm by email" is
not yet backed by any code path.

**Verdict: Accurate design, one overclaim in the response copy.** The privacy policy draft
describes deletion as logged and handled by a person, matching what is actually true, and does
not promise an automatic email until one is built.

## Cross-cutting findings not tied to a single document

- **Footer only links to Privacy and Terms** (`components/layout/footer.tsx` lines 46 to 49).
  Advertiser Terms is not linked from the footer at all despite being a live, indexable-by-link
  page. Once the new documents publish, the footer needs a "Legal" column update; that edit is
  out of scope for this lane (it touches `components/`, owned by another lane) but is called out
  here so it is not lost.
- **Two active promo codes, two different programs.** FINDMYMAHJGAME (Directory Membership,
  providers/teachers, `/join` and `/get-listed`) and FINDMYMAHJ (sponsor/advertiser track,
  `/advertise`, 6 months free plus 30% off for life). Neither is explained in any policy
  document. `docs/policy/drafts/billing-disclosures.md` covers FINDMYMAHJGAME only, since that
  is the one tied to the $89/year Directory Membership named in the mission. FINDMYMAHJ and the
  advertiser/sponsor track are a separate product surface and should get their own disclosure
  when that lane is scoped; noted so it is not mistaken for an oversight.
- **`docs/membership-system-spec.md` is itself stale relative to the live product.** Its opening
  note (lines 1 to 3) already flags that the pricing name changed once (from "Pro" to "Verified
  Community Leader" at $12/month or $99/year), but the live site has since moved past that too:
  `/join` now shows a single $89/year "Directory Membership" with "Charter Member" and "Founding
  Advisor" recognition tiers, not "Verified Community Leader." The spec document was not edited
  for this audit (out of scope, owned by another lane), but any reader should treat `/join`
  (`app/join/page.tsx`) as the current source of truth for pricing, not the spec.

## [OWNER TO CONFIRM] list

Collected in one place; each also appears inline in the relevant draft.

1. **Business entity name** for the "who you are agreeing with" line in Terms of Use, Privacy
   Policy, and Provider Terms. Not in the codebase anywhere.
2. **Governing law and jurisdiction** for dispute resolution. Not in the codebase anywhere.
3. **Contact/registered address**, if one is required for the Privacy Policy or Terms. Not in
   the codebase; the only contact point in code is the email address hello@findmymahjgame.com.
4. **The 6-months-versus-12-months free period conflict** described in section 6. Which is the
   real policy: 6 months, no card, manual billing setup by email (what is live today), or a full
   year, card collected at checkout, automatic renewal (what is built but dark)? Or does the
   automated path only apply to new members who join after `launch_payments` flips, while
   already-onboarded Charter Members keep the 6-month manual deal? This changes what
   `docs/policy/drafts/billing-disclosures.md` can honestly promise.
5. **Whether FINDMYMAHJ (advertiser/sponsor track) and FINDMYMAHJGAME (Directory Membership)
   are meant to be two permanently separate paid products**, or whether Advertiser Terms should
   be retired in favor of one unified billing disclosure. Affects whether
   `app/advertiser-terms/page.tsx` gets rewritten or kept as a narrower, separate document.
6. **Whether the 7-day removal SLA in the current Privacy Policy is an operational commitment
   the owner can actually meet**, since nothing in code enforces it. Keep, loosen, or tie it to
   the `account_events` deletion-request log becoming a tracked queue.
7. **The 14-day grace period, dunning sequence, and automatic revert-to-free-basic policy**
   described in project memory needs to either get built before the billing disclosure goes
   live describing it as current behavior, or the disclosure needs to describe it as intended
   future behavior rather than present tense. This is an engineering dependency, not just a
   wording choice; it should not block drafting, but it must block publishing the billing
   disclosure as literally true.

## What should block a launch gate flip

See `docs/policy/launch-gate.md` for the full checklist. The one finding from this audit that is
a genuine blocker, not just a documentation gap: **`playerMatching` should not flip until
`readMatchingConsent()` (or an equivalent fail-closed check) is actually called from every route
that can create, propose, or accept a match.** Right now the consent contract exists in code but
is unproven to run. Flipping the gate on the strength of the drafted community standards alone,
without that wiring confirmed, would make the published document false the moment it goes live.
