## What Find My Mahj Game is

Find My Mahj Game is a national directory and matching platform for mahjong players, teachers,
and organizers. We help people find games, teachers, events, and each other. We do not organize
events, operate venues, or provide mahjong instruction ourselves, and we are not responsible for
the accuracy of listings or the conduct of the people and businesses listed here.

The site is general audience. Anyone can browse listings, create a free player or provider
listing, and use search. One part of the site, Mahj Match, is limited to adults 18 and over; see
"Mahj Match and matching" below.

## Accounts

Creating an account uses a passwordless sign-in: you enter your email, we send you a one-time
link, and clicking it signs you in. There is no password to create, remember, or reset.

You are responsible for keeping access to your own email address secure, since that is what
controls your account. You can deactivate your account at any time from your account page. You
can also request deletion of your account; see "Your data" below and our Privacy Policy for how
that works.

- You are responsible for the accuracy of the information in your listing.

## Mahj Match and matching

Mahj Match helps players find and form a table with other players based on location,
availability, and preferences. Mahj Match is available only to adults 18 years of age and
older. Before you can be matched, you must:

1. have an active account,
2. affirmatively confirm you are 18 or older, and
3. explicitly opt in to matching.

We do not share your last name, your email address, or your exact location with another player
through matching. Once a table is proposed, we share only your first name and the compatibility
facts you already agreed to share (things like your general area, availability, and skill
preference) with the other people on that proposed table. Nothing more is shared until you both
say yes.

Full detail on how matching works, and the community standards that apply to it, live in a
separate document, our Matching Community Standards at [OWNER TO CONFIRM final URL].

## Meeting other players in person: your safety

Find My Mahj Game is a directory and matching platform. What happens when you meet another
player, whether through a listing you found yourself or a table Mahj Match proposed, is between
you and that person. We do not run background checks, verify identities, or supervise in-person
meetings.

By using this platform to connect with other players, you agree that:

- Find My Mahj Game is not a party to any in-person meeting or interaction between users.
- We do not screen, verify, background check, or endorse any individual on this platform.
- You are solely responsible for your own safety when meeting anyone you connect with through
  this site.
- We strongly recommend meeting in a public place the first time, letting someone know where you
  are going, and trusting your instincts.
- We are not liable for harm, loss, injury, or disputes arising from in-person meetings between
  users.
- Disputes between players are between those players. We have no obligation to intervene or
  mediate, though we do want to know if someone made you feel unsafe; see "Blocking and
  reporting" above.

**Safety tip:** meet new players in public places, coffee shops, libraries, community centers,
restaurants. Never share your home address with someone you have not met before, and never
include one in a public listing.
