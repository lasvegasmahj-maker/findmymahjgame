## Mahj Match and matching

Mahj Match helps players find and form a table with other players based on location,
availability, and preferences. Mahj Match is available only to adults 18 years of age and
older. Before you can be matched, you must:

1. have an active account,
2. affirmatively confirm you are 18 or older, and
3. explicitly opt in to matching.

We do not share your last name, your email address, or your exact location with another player
through matching. Once a table is proposed, we share only your first name and the compatibility
facts you already agreed to share (things like your general area, availability, and skill
preference) with the other people on that proposed table. Nothing more is shared until you both
say yes.

Full detail on how matching works, and the community standards that apply to it, live in a
separate document, our [Matching Community Standards](/matching-standards).
