# Founder Daily Operating Checklist (Launch)

Your 15-minute daily routine for the first 30 days. Same order every day, fewest clicks. Most days you will be done in less than 10 minutes. The goal is simple: keep the listings clean, get real games approved, answer humans fast, and protect the promise that money never crosses the table.

Do this once in the morning. If something big comes in later (a match to approve, an inquiry), handle it when you see the email; do not wait for tomorrow.

Set up once: bookmark findmymahjgame.com/admin on your phone and your laptop. Sign in. Everything below starts there.

---

## The 15-minute routine, in order

### 1. Open /admin and read the banner (1 minute)

The pink bar at the top tells you what needs you today: pending listings, new inquiries, new ambassador applications. If there is no pink bar, there is nothing waiting. Read the "Today's focus" box right under it; that is your top 5 tasks pulled from your task list.

If the banner is empty and your inbox is quiet, you are done. Close the laptop. A quiet day early in launch is normal and fine.

### 2. Approve or reject pending listings (3-5 minutes)

Click the Players, Venues, and Events tabs in turn. Pending rows sit at the top. For each one, ask:

- Is it real and does the link work? (Click the website or registration link. If it is dead or empty, Reject.)
- Does it belong? No Nevada teachers (Las Vegas stays community-only, players go to Las Vegas Mahjong). No open play with no schedule. No row with no clear source.

Approve the good ones, Reject the rest. A yellow "Flagged" row is one the link checker held back; open it, check the link yourself, then Approve or Reject individually.

Bulk tip: if you have a big batch, use "Select all pending" then "Approve Selected." If you are approving more than 10 at once, the screen reminds you to run the link check first. Flagged rows are never swept into bulk approve, so you cannot accidentally publish a bad link.

### 3. Approve any table matches (when one arrives, 1 minute)

When a table is ready to form, you get an email to hello@findmymahjgame.com titled "Match ready for your approval." Open it, read the players and the day/time, and click Approve this match or Skip. Nothing reaches a player until you approve. Approving emails each player a private link to claim their seat. You will land back on /admin with a green confirmation. (Early in launch these will be rare and Dallas-only. That is by design.)

### 4. Answer inquiries and ambassador applications (3-5 minutes)

Inquiries tab: anyone who used the contact or advertise form is here, newest first. Reply same day if you can; people remember a fast, warm reply. Click their email to write back, then mark the row Replied. Rows tagged "Action needed: review and create listing" are advertisers or venues asking to be listed; reply, then add their listing when they confirm.

Ambassadors tab: read any new application. If she is a fit, click Approve and send her the Founding Ambassador welcome in your voice. If you need more first, click Contacted and email her. Move every new one out of "new" so the banner clears.

Newsletter note: every signup also emails hello@ and lands in your inbox, so you never lose one even before Mailchimp is fully wired. No action needed daily; just confirm they are flowing in.

### 5. Glance at the North Star and confirmations (1-2 minutes)

Click North Star Metrics. Look at four numbers: Created, Filled, Played, Recurring. You are not filling anything in today (that is the Friday review); you are just watching the trend and catching anything stuck. The one ratio to protect: of the games we ask about, are at least half coming back "Yes, we played"? Tables that fill but never confirm a real game are the early warning sign. If Played stays flat while Filled climbs, that is your signal to personally check in with a host or two.

### 6. Send today's one outreach (the rest of your time)

Pick one relationship to move forward today. The list lives in your CEO OS Drive (Tier 1 Outreach, Teacher Tracker, Warm Relationship Activation Log). One real message to one real person beats ten cold ones. For a teacher or venue you want to claim their listing, open their row, click "Claim link," and paste it into your email. That is the highest-value thing you can do most days.

That is the routine. Banner, listings, matches, people, metrics, one outreach.

## What you check vs. what you only approve

- You APPROVE: every listing, every table match, every ambassador. Nothing goes public or reaches a player without your click. That is the whole safety model; keep it.
- You only CHECK (no action most days): the metrics funnel, the confirmation rate, the flow of newsletter signups and inquiries.
- You SEND when it matters: same-day replies to humans, one outreach a day, and the ambassador welcome when you approve one.

## Daily guardrails (never break these)

- Money never crosses the table. No booking fees, no pay-for-placement, no Find My Mahj Game lessons. If anyone asks you to take a cut, the answer is no.
- No Nevada teachers in the public teacher listing. Las Vegas is players, hosts, tables, and community only.
- Real and sourced only. If you cannot click a link and see it load, do not publish it. If you cannot say where a listing came from, hold it.
- No fake counts, no fake names. Never tell anyone "47 players in Texas" unless that is a real, confirmed number.
- A table is private. Player names and near-full tables never show on a public page; that is already built, just never undo it.

## When to escalate to Jason

- A page is down, a route shows an error, or a link in the admin is broken.
- The site asks for a password it never asked for before, or a form stops submitting.
- Anything about deploys, keys, or Vercel. You run the business; he runs the backend.

## What is NOT in your daily 15 minutes

- Filling in the metrics scorecard. That is the separate 15-minute Friday review (growth/ambassadors/10-founder-weekly-dashboard.md). Do that once a week, not daily.
- The one-time launch-day deploy steps. Those live in LAUNCH_GATE.md and run once, not every day.

## One-line version (tape this to your monitor)

Open /admin. Clear the pink banner: approve good listings, reject dead ones, answer every human, approve any match. Glance at North Star. Send one outreach. Done.
