# Find My Mahj Game: Support Workflow

How inbound to hello@findmymahjgame.com gets triaged, answered, and turned into tasks. Ready to use at launch. Keep it open in a tab during launch week.

Last updated: 2026-06-12. Owner: Shauna. Review weekly for the first month, then monthly.

---

## The one rule that governs every reply

Every message gets a real human reply within 1-2 business days. That is the promise on the live site (contact page and FAQ), so it is a commitment, not a goal. A warm, fast, plain reply from a real person is the whole brand. When in doubt, reply sooner and shorter.

Voice: warm, plain, like a friend who runs the thing. No jargon. No em dashes. No emoji. Active voice. Sign as Shauna.

---

## Where inbound comes from

Three streams all land in or near this one inbox:

1. The contact form at /contact. It posts through Formspree and arrives as an email with a topic already selected from this fixed list: Player listing question, Advertising inquiry, Venue listing, Event listing, Partnership opportunity, Bug or website issue, Other. The topic does most of your triage for you.
2. Direct email to hello@findmymahjgame.com (links on /contact, /faq, /teachers, and footer).
3. The Monday automated check-in digest (site health, pending listings, gaps). This is not a customer message. Do not reply to it. Skim it, pull any action items into tasks, archive.

All outbound replies send from hello@findmymahjgame.com.

---

## The daily routine (about 15 minutes)

Do this once a day, morning, during launch month. Twice a day in launch week.

1. Open the inbox. Sort newest first.
2. For each new message, do the four-step triage below.
3. Reply or file a task. Never leave a customer message with no reply and no task.
4. Apply a label (see labels) so nothing gets lost.
5. Archive what is fully handled.

Inbox zero is the goal each day. A message is "done" only when it has a reply AND, if needed, a task.

---

## Four-step triage (run on every message)

Step 1. Is it a real person or noise?
Spam, sales pitches to us, and obvious junk get no reply. Label Spam, archive. Do not engage.

Step 2. Which bucket? Match it to one of the eight buckets below. The contact-form topic usually tells you. For direct emails, read the first two lines and pick the closest bucket.

Step 3. Can I answer from a template in under two minutes?
If yes, send the matching reply below (edit the name and any specifics), label it, done.
If no, see step 4.

Step 4. Does it need an action beyond the reply (approve a listing, fix a bug, follow up, a decision)?
If yes, send a holding reply AND create a task (see "What becomes a task"). The reply and the task are both required.

---

## Labels (use these exact names)

- Listings (player, group, instructor, venue, event sign-ups and changes)
- Advertising (paid inquiries and partnerships)
- Bug (something on the site is broken)
- Safety (anything about a person, a meeting, money, or feeling unsafe)
- Press-Partner (media, podcasts, collaborations)
- Founder-Decision (needs Shauna to decide something new)
- Done
- Spam

A message can carry two labels (for example Listings + Done).

## The eight buckets, with ready-to-send replies

Copy, paste, change the name, send. Each reply already follows the brand rules.

### Bucket 1. Player listing question (most common)
What it looks like: "How do I list myself?" "When does my listing go live?" "Can I change my city?"

If asking how to list:
> Hi [Name],
>
> So glad you want to get on the map. It takes about two minutes and it is free. Go to findmymahjgame.com/list-my-game, fill out the short form, and I will review and approve it within 1-2 business days. Once it is live, players in your area can find you on your state page.
>
> Reply here if you hit any snag. Happy to help.
>
> Shauna

If asking to update or remove a listing (this is a task, see below):
> Hi [Name],
>
> Happy to take care of that. I handle all changes by hand right now, so tell me your name as it appears on the listing and exactly what you want changed or removed, and I will update it within 1-2 business days and confirm here.
>
> Shauna

### Bucket 2. Group, club, or open-play host
What it looks like: "Can I list my Tuesday group?"

> Hi [Name],
>
> Yes, and thank you for hosting. Go to findmymahjgame.com/list-my-game and choose Group / Open Play Host as your listing type. Add your day, time, and how people can reach you so newcomers know how to join. I will review it within 1-2 business days.
>
> If you want help getting your regulars listed too, just say the word.
>
> Shauna

### Bucket 3. Instructor or teacher
What it looks like: "I teach mahjong. Can I be listed?"

Brand guardrail: everywhere outside Nevada we welcome and promote every teacher equally. In Nevada we focus on games, open plays, and community, not competing teacher listings, because our founder also runs Las Vegas Mahjong and we are open about that. Never imply we take a cut of lessons. Money never crosses the table.

> Hi [Name],
>
> Yes, and we would love to have you. Create a free listing at findmymahjgame.com/list-my-game and mark that you offer lessons. That is free and always will be. If you want a featured spotlight placement later, those are available on the advertise page, but it is never required and we never take a cut of your lessons.
>
> I will review your listing within 1-2 business days.
>
> Shauna

(If the teacher is in Nevada, add a warm line: "Quick note for Nevada specifically: we keep our focus there on games and community rather than teacher listings, and we are open about why on our FAQ. I would still love to point local players your way through groups and open plays.")

### Bucket 4. Event or tournament listing
What it looks like: "Can you list our tournament?"

> Hi [Name],
>
> We would love to help players find it. Event and tournament listings run through our advertise page at findmymahjgame.com/advertise. Send me the event name, dates, city, a link, and a short description, and I will get back to you with options within 1-2 business days.
>
> Shauna

(Then create a task to follow up with details, see below.)

### Bucket 5. Advertising inquiry (paid)
What it looks like: "How much to advertise?" "We sell mahjong sets, can we sponsor?"

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for thinking of us. Our advertising and sponsorship options are on findmymahjgame.com/advertise, including banner placements, sponsored state pages, and event listings that reach mahjong players nationwide. Tell me a bit about your business and what you have in mind, and I will follow up with specifics within 1-2 business days.
>
> Shauna

(Create a task: Advertising follow-up.)

### Bucket 6. Bug or website issue
What it looks like: "The map will not load." "I cannot submit the form." "My state page is empty."

First, check if it is real. "Why is my state page empty?" is not a bug; it is expected at launch (we are building from scratch). Answer with the FAQ line:
> Hi [Name],
>
> Good question. We are building the directory from scratch, so some states have few listings yet. You can be the first. Create a free listing at findmymahjgame.com/list-my-game and invite your local group to do the same. The page fills in as players join.
>
> Shauna

If it is a real bug (broken page, form will not submit, image broken):
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for flagging this, that is really helpful. I am looking into it now and will let you know the moment it is fixed. If you can tell me what device and browser you were using, that speeds things up.
>
> Shauna

(Create a Bug task. Loop in Jason for anything code-level.)

### Bucket 7. Partnership, press, or media
What it looks like: podcast invites, collaboration offers, journalists.

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for reaching out, I would genuinely love to talk. Tell me a little more about what you have in mind and your timeline, and I will get back to you personally within 1-2 business days.
>
> Shauna

(Label Press-Partner. Create a follow-up task. Flag to Shauna directly if time-sensitive.)

### Bucket 8. Safety or sensitive (highest priority, always)
What it looks like: anyone reports feeling unsafe, a bad interaction, money being asked for, a person to be removed, or anything involving a meeting between players.

This jumps the line. Reply same day. Never auto-respond with a template alone; read it carefully first.

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thank you for telling me, and I am glad you did. Your safety matters more than anything else here. I am looking into this right now. Can you share any detail you are comfortable sharing? I will act on it today and follow up with you personally.
>
> Shauna

(Label Safety + Founder-Decision. Create a high-priority task. Remember the core promise: money never crosses the table. If anyone is asking players for money to join a game, that is a removal, not a maybe.)

## What becomes a task (and what does not)

Most messages end at "reply and archive." A message becomes a task only when it needs an action after the reply. Log tasks in the admin tasks view at /admin/tasks (or your running task list) so nothing slips.

Becomes a task:
- Approve, update, or remove a listing (player, group, instructor, venue, event). Listings are reviewed and published by hand, target 1-2 business days.
- Advertising or event inquiry that needs a custom follow-up.
- A real bug to fix (assign to Jason if code-level).
- Partnership or press that needs a real conversation or scheduling.
- Anything Safety related (always a task, always high priority).
- Anything that needs a new decision from Shauna (label Founder-Decision).

Does NOT become a task (reply and done):
- "How do I list?" and other how-to questions answered by a template.
- "Why is my state empty?" and other FAQ-answered questions.
- Thank-you notes and general praise (reply warmly, archive).
- Spam (no reply, archive).

Task format, keep it one line:
`[Bucket] Person name, city, what they need, by when.`
Example: `[Listings] Carol R., Plano TX, approve new player listing, by Thu.`

## Priority order when several land at once

1. Safety (same day, always first).
2. Bugs that block sign-ups or break a live page (same day).
3. Listing approvals (these are why people came; keep the 1-2 day promise).
4. Advertising and partnership follow-ups.
5. Everything else.

---

## Escalate to Jason when

- Anything code-level: form not submitting, a page erroring, the map broken, email not sending.
- Anything touching data privacy, the database, or admin access.
Give him the exact message, the URL, and the device and browser if you have it.

## Escalate to Shauna as a Founder-Decision when

- A new partnership, sponsorship rate, or press opportunity needs a yes or no.
- A safety situation needs a judgment call.
- Anything that would change a published rule (pricing, the teacher promise, what we list where).

---

## Three things to never do in support

1. Never imply we take a cut of any teacher's lessons or any game. Money never crosses the table.
2. Never quote a player, group, venue, or event count from the site as if it were live demand. The directory is new and filling in. Be honest and inviting about that.
3. Never use an em dash or an emoji in a reply. Commas, periods, and parentheses only. ASCII hyphen for ranges (1-2 business days).

## Weekly five-minute review (do every Monday after the digest)

- Any message older than 2 business days with no reply? Clear it now.
- Same question three times this week? Add it to the FAQ at /faq so the site answers it for you.
- Any bug reported more than once? Make it Jason's top task.
- Tally counts per bucket in a note. After a few weeks the pattern tells you what to automate or templatize next.
