## The one rule that protects everything

Read this before you send a single message.

**Money never crosses the table.** We never stand between a teacher and her students. Students book on the teacher's own site. No booking cuts, no paid placement, ever. This is permanent. It is also our single biggest selling point to teachers, so say it early and say it plainly.

**The Teacher Promise (use this wording when a teacher asks "what is the catch"):**
> Find My Mahj Game never stands between you and your students. They book on your own site, and money never crosses the table. No booking cuts, no paid placement, ever.

**The Las Vegas guardrail (do not break this).** Las Vegas is community-only and is Shauna's other business, Las Vegas Mahjong. We do NOT recruit or list competing Nevada teachers on Find My Mahj Game. If a great teacher is in Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, or anywhere in Nevada, she is not a teacher-partner target here. Send her to Las Vegas Mahjong instead. The /teachers page already excludes Nevada automatically; keep outreach consistent with that.

## Who to contact, in order

Work the warmest, closest-to-market people first. Stop at the line. Do not cold-pitch your way down a list when a warm note up top converts in one reply.

**Tier 1: Shauna's own Dallas-area teaching circle and mentors (today).**
The teachers and mentors Shauna already knows and trusts in Dallas-Fort Worth. Warmest relationship, real rosters, and they sit in our proof market. A Founding Teacher invite goes to these people first. These are personal notes, not a sequence.

**Tier 2: Warm conference and network relationships in Texas (this week).**
Teachers, organizers, and studio owners Shauna met at conferences or through the network who are in DFW, San Antonio, Houston, or Austin. Friendly note that references how you met. (Posture with the broader Oh My Mahjong network is deter then partner: we lead with our own Dallas mentors, and the wider OMM partnership conversation comes later, with receipts. Do not pitch the whole OMM network in week one.)

**Tier 3: Texas teachers we have not met (this week, in parallel).**
Anyone teaching American Mahjong in our launch metros. Find them through senior centers, community centers, library event calendars, the JCC, active-adult communities, and parks-and-rec class listings. This is the polite cold note.

**Tier 4: Teachers in or near future launch cities (weeks 2-4).**
Strong teachers just outside Texas who can seed the next markets. A slightly colder ask, so reach them after you have one or two happy testimonials in hand.

**Never:** competing Nevada teachers. See the guardrail above.

**Quick ranking inside any tier.** Score each person 1-3 on three things, work the highest totals first:
- Warmth (3 = personal relationship, 2 = one introduction or shared event, 1 = cold)
- Roster (3 = a class running now, 2 = past classes, 1 = unknown)
- Geography (3 = DFW, 2 = San Antonio / Houston / Austin, 1 = elsewhere in Texas)

A 9 gets a same-day personal note from Shauna. A 5 or below waits.

## The ask (keep it this small)

Lead with what helps the teacher, never with what we need. The first ask is tiny:

> A free listing as a trusted teacher so your students can find you, and a simple link you can hand every student who finishes a class so they can find a regular game right away. No cost. You control what we share. That is the whole ask.

Do not ask for an ambassador commitment, a quota, or anything paid in the first message. The bigger asks come after she says yes.

## Follow-up cadence

Match the pace to how warm she is. Never let a teacher who said yes but has not been listed sit longer than a week without a friendly nudge; that gap is where partnerships leak.

**Tier 1, Dallas mentors (Hot):**
- Day 0: personal note or text from Shauna (Email 1A or DM 1).
- Day 2-3: if quiet, a one-line personal nudge.
- On any reply: book the call, aim to list her and hand off her student link the same week.
- Weekly friendly touch until she is listed and sharing her link.

**Tier 2-3, warm and cold Texas teachers:**
- Day 0: Email 1A (warm) or 1B (cold), or DM equivalent.
- Day 3-4: Email 2 follow-up.
- Day 8: one last short follow-up, either a different angle (offer to feature her in the first newsletter) or a simple "should I close your spot for now?"
- If still silent after Day 8: move to a monthly check-in. Do not keep chasing; founder time is the scarcest resource.

**Tier 4, future-city teachers:**
- Same Day 0 / Day 4 / Day 8 rhythm, but start only after you have one or two testimonials to include. A warm quote from a listed Texas teacher does more than a third follow-up.

**Across all tiers:**
- A teacher who replies eagerly jumps to Hot, move immediately.
- Once she says "I am in," verify and list her within the week, then send the offer confirmation and her student link. That fast turnaround is what earns referrals.

## What to do the moment a teacher says yes

1. Confirm she currently teaches American Mahjong and that you have a real two-way reply (not just a form fill).
2. Confirm in writing that she is fine with public-venue, first-names-only, no-home-address listings.
3. List her with first name and areas served only. Never her home address or personal phone.
4. Send the offer confirmation (Email 3 wording) and her student handoff link.
5. Set a 14-day check-in: are her students finding games, is she sharing the link. (The deeper vetting and incentive ladder live in growth/ambassadors/44-teacher-acquisition-blitz.md.)
