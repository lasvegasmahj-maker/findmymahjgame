## Part 2. Three short personal templates per segment

Three segments, three templates each: first touch, day-3 nudge, and the reply close. Keep them short. The point is one true, specific line at the top, then one clear ask. Replace the bracketed tokens before sending. Send from a real person at hello@findmymahjgame.com so replies reach a human.

The one rule that makes these work: add one real detail near the top (her city, a class she runs, a post you saw, where you met). One true line beats a page of polish. Never invent the detail.

## Part 3. Status tracking scheme (maps to what you already have)

Nothing new to build. Track each contact's status in /admin/crm, and let the follow-up live as tasks in /admin/tasks. Here is the exact mapping.

### Status in the CRM (one field per contact)

These are the statuses the CRM already supports. Move each contact along as you work.

| Status | Means | Your next move |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| not_contacted | On the list, not yet reached | Send first-touch template |
| contacted | First touch sent | Wait 3 days, then day-3 nudge |
| replied | She wrote back | Send the reply-close template, then call |
| claimed | She claimed her listing or applied as ambassador | Onboard her (graduation kit within 14 days) |
| activated | She started a real table or shared her link | Weekly check-in until the table is recurring |
| declined | Said no for now | Thank her, leave the door open, stop the sequence |
| do_not_contact | Hold (for example San Antonio until Kristel-vs-Crystal) | Do not send anything |

The only status that counts as a win is activated, and behind it, a recurring table. Claimed-but-not-activated is not progress.

### Follow-up as Founder Tasks (so nothing slips)

When you send a first touch and set a contact to contacted, also create one task in /admin/tasks so the nudge does not get forgotten:

- Category: outreach for the first touch, follow-up for the day-3 nudge.
- Task text: short and named, for example "Day-3 nudge: Ashley Schroeder (Blossom and Bamboo)".
- Related name: the contact's name (the tasks page already shows this beside the task).
- Due date: today plus 3 days.

The tasks page already surfaces high-priority and due items in "Today's focus," so the nudges will rise to the top on the right day.

### The simple daily rhythm (10-day launch)

1. Open /admin/crm, filter to wave 2, sort by rank.
2. Pick the top 5 still at not_contacted. Send the matching segment template (one real personalization line each).
3. Set each to contacted. Add a "Day-3 nudge" follow-up task due in 3 days.
4. Check /admin/tasks "Today's focus" for any nudges due today. Send them.
5. Anyone who replied: set to replied, send the reply close, and call. Move toward activated.

A good launch-week target: 5 first touches a day, every reply answered same day, and the Wave 1 anchors all called by phone in the first two days. Keep San Antonio held until the one founder text comes back.
