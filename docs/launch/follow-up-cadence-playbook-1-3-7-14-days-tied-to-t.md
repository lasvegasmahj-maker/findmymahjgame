# Find My Mahj Game: Follow-up Cadence Playbook (1/3/7/14)

A simple, repeatable way to follow up so no warm contact goes cold during launch. One cadence, four touches, ready-to-send copy. It plugs straight into the Founder Tasks view at /admin/tasks (the "follow-up" category and the "Follow-ups" view).

Read this once. After that, the only thing you do is open Founder Tasks, work the Follow-ups that are due, and tap Done.

---

## The rule in one line

After any first contact (a call, a text, an email, a booth conversation), you owe that person exactly four more touches: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14. If they reply or say yes at any point, you stop the cadence and move to the next step. If they say "not now," you stop and leave the door open.

Four touches. Then you let it rest. That is the whole system.

---

## Why these four days

- Day 1: catch them while the conversation is still warm. A short thank-you and one clear next step.
- Day 3: add value, not pressure. Show how simple it is or send the one link they need.
- Day 7: make the ask plainly. Offer a 15-minute call or the one action that moves them forward.
- Day 14: the gracious close. No guilt, door stays open, easy to say yes later.

Most yeses come on Day 1 or Day 7. Day 3 keeps you in their inbox without nagging. Day 14 protects the relationship so a "no for now" can still become a "yes in August."

---

## How it lives in Founder Tasks (/admin/tasks)

You do not need a separate tracker. The Founder Tasks view already has a "follow-up" category. Use it.

When you finish a first contact with someone:

1. In Founder Tasks, add a task. Set the category to "follow-up."
2. Put the person in the task line and the related_name field, for example: "Follow-up 1: Lisa Rocchio (Charleston Club)."
3. Set the due date to tomorrow (Day 1).
4. In Notes, paste who they are, what you talked about, and one true personal detail to use next time.

When Day 1 is done, tap Done and add the next one with the due date set out the right number of days (Day 3, then Day 7, then Day 14). Set high priority for hot contacts so they show up in "Today's focus" at the top of the page.

Naming convention so the Follow-ups view stays scannable:

- Follow-up 1: {Name} ({org or city}) -> due Day 1
- Follow-up 2: {Name} ({org or city}) -> due Day 3
- Follow-up 3: {Name} ({org or city}) -> due Day 7
- Follow-up 4: {Name} ({org or city}) -> due Day 14

If you are waiting on them to do something (send a date, confirm a seat), use the Waiting button and note who you are waiting on. It moves to "Waiting on" so it stops cluttering your open list, and you can nudge from there.

Daily habit: open Founder Tasks once a day, work everything in "Today's focus," and clear the follow-ups that are due. That is the founder follow-up routine in five minutes.

## Before you send anything

- Replace the tokens: {{first}} (their first name), {{city}} (their city), {{detail}} (one true, specific thing, such as a club they run, a comment they made, the students they teach).
- One real line of personalization beats a page of polish. If you cannot name a true detail, you do not know them well enough to send Touch 2 yet. Go back to Notes.
- Stop the cadence the moment they reply. Switch to the reply snippet at the bottom.
- Keep it in your voice. These are starting points, not scripts to read word for word.

---

## The copy: four touches

The copy below is written for the most common launch follow-up: a teacher, host, venue, or organizer you want on the platform before Dallas Welcome Week. Light variants for each type follow the core set. Find My Mahj Game is free, money never crosses the table, and new groups always meet in a public place for the first game. Lead with those when in doubt.

### Touch 1 (Day 1): thank you and the one next step

Subject: Great talking, {{first}}
Alternate subject: So glad we connected, {{first}}

Hi {{first}},

It was so good to talk. {{detail}} stuck with me.

Quick recap of what Find My Mahj Game is, so it is in one place: it is a free service that helps people find a regular mahjong game near them. Players find each other, claim seats at a table, and meet in a public place for the first game. There is no cost to anyone, and money never crosses the table.

Here is the one next step: {{single clear action, for example: reply with a good time for a 15-minute call this week, or claim your free teacher listing at findmymahjgame.com/teachers}}.

Warmly,
Shauna
Find My Mahj Game

### Touch 2 (Day 3): make it easy, add value

For you, it is a few minutes and it runs from your phone. Whenever you are ready, here is the one step: {{the same single link or action from Touch 1}}.

Warmly,
Shauna

Could we do 15 minutes this week? I will walk you through it and, if it feels right, we can set up your free listing or start a first table together while we are on the phone. No prep needed on your end.

If a call is not your thing, just reply here and I will sort it out by email. Either way, here is the one step in the meantime: {{the same single link or action}}.

Warmly,
Shauna

Subject: No rush, {{first}}, the door stays open
Alternate subject: Whenever the timing is right

I will leave you in peace after this one. I know inboxes fill up, and there is no deadline here.

I would still love to have you part of Find My Mahj Game in {{city}} whenever the timing is right, even months from now. The link will be the same and I will be glad to pick it up then: {{the same single link or action}}.

It was a real pleasure connecting. I hope our paths cross over a table soon.

Warmly,
Shauna
Find My Mahj Game

## Light variants by contact type

Swap one line near the top of each touch to fit who you are talking to. Keep everything else the same.

- Teacher: "Your students already trust you, and Find My Mahj Game gives them a real game to keep playing in after class. Your free teacher listing is at findmymahjgame.com/teachers."
- Venue or host: "Your space is already a gathering place in {{city}}. A few standing tables a week is more reasons for people to come through your doors, at no cost to you."
- Club organizer: "If you already run a club, your members can fill a new table in a day, and the site handles the coordinating for you."
- Ambassador prospect: "Founding Ambassadors help a few players start tables and fill seats. It takes a few minutes from your phone, and every table from your link is credited to you. Apply at findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors." (For full ambassador recruiting, use the longer 5-email sequence in growth/ambassadors/02-recruiting-email-sequence.md.)

## When they reply: stop the cadence

The moment someone replies with interest, stop the scheduled touches and send this. Mark the follow-up task Done and add the real next step (the call, the listing, the first table).

Subject: Wonderful, {{first}}

Hi {{first}},

This made my day. Let us get you set up.

The one thing to do now is {{the single next step: book the call, claim the listing, start the table}}. It takes a couple of minutes, and I am right here if anything is unclear. Just reply.

So glad to have you.

Warmly,
Shauna
Find My Mahj Game

If they reply with a question instead of a clear yes: answer the one question in two or three sentences, then close with the same single next step. Do not pile on. One question, one answer, one ask.

If they reply "not right now": thank them warmly, tell them the door stays open, and stop the cadence. For example: "Totally understood, {{first}}. The link will be here whenever the timing is right, and it was great connecting."

Rules that never change:
- Free for players. Money never crosses the table.
- First game is always in a public place.
- One personal, true detail in every touch.
- Stop the cadence the instant they reply.
- Four touches, then let it rest.

## Founder Tasks setup, in plain steps

1. Open /admin/tasks. Filter to the "follow-up" category to see your Follow-ups.
2. For each new contact, add four tasks named Follow-up 1 through 4, dated Day 1, 3, 7, and 14, with their name in related_name.
3. Mark high priority for hot contacts (Las Vegas and Dallas relationships, anyone who can start a table this month) so they surface in "Today's focus."
4. Work the due ones once a day. Tap Done. The next one is already dated.
5. If you are waiting on them, hit Waiting and note who you are waiting on. Nudge from the "Waiting on" list.

That is the system. Open it daily, work what is due, and no warm contact slips through the cracks during launch.
