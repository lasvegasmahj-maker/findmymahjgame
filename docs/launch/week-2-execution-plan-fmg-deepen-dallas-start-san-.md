## PRIORITY 3: FRESHNESS DISCIPLINE (the trust engine)

A free directory lives or dies on whether players trust it. One dead game, one game that ended months ago, and a player drives across town to an empty room and never comes back. Freshness is not housekeeping. It is the product.

The site already has the machinery. Week 2 is about running it as a daily habit.

### How the freshness system works (so you can run it)
- Every event and venue listing carries two clocks: confirmed_active_at (last time someone confirmed it is still running) and ended_reports (a count of people who said it ended).
- From /admin you can issue three magic links per listing: claim, still-running, and ended (each good for 90 days, because this audience reopens emails for weeks). The still-running and ended links are how you ask hosts and players to keep listings honest.
- When someone clicks still-running, the listing's freshness clock resets and it sorts higher. When someone clicks ended, the count goes up and, on the very first ended report, you get an email at hello@findmymahjgame.com so you can verify before it disappoints a player.
- Tables get an automatic "did you play?" ask after they fill or age past the cutoff. That confirmation is what feeds the North Star. It is the same loop that turns a filled table into Player-Games Confirmed.

### One-time setup the founder must clear (blocks the edits queue and confirm flow)
- The claims-and-freshness database migration must be pasted once in the Supabase SQL editor: supabase/migrations/2026-06-12-claims-freshness.sql. Until it is pasted, the Edit Requests page at /admin/edits shows a yellow "one-time setup" banner and teacher edits and freshness confirmations cannot land. This is the unlock for the entire claim-and-freshness motion. Paste it Monday morning before you send any claim links.

### The daily freshness habit (10 minutes, every weekday)
1. Open /admin/today. Read the North Star and the confirmation rate first.
2. Open /admin/edits. Approve or reject any pending teacher edits. Every approved edit is a teacher keeping her own listing fresh for you, which is exactly what we want.
3. Check hello@ for any "Listing reported ended" email. Verify it the same day. If it is gone, flag it in /admin and remove it. If it is fine, leave it; the count is bounded and will not spam you.
4. Send a few still-running asks. Pick 3 to 5 listings older than two weeks, send each host her still-running link, and ask her to tap it if her game is still on. Aim for 15 or more confirmations across the week.

### The weekly freshness sweep (Friday)
- Sort listings by confirmed_active_at. Anything not confirmed in 30 days gets a still-running ask. Anything not confirmed in 45 days and unreachable comes down. An empty page is better than a wrong one.
- Confirm every /t/CODE on /play still loads and every listed event is still happening. Safety pass at the same time: public venues only, first names only in public, no home addresses, no personal phone numbers anywhere.
