# City Launch Plan: Boca Raton / Palm Beach

Third proof market. This area has some of the deepest mahjong and teacher concentrations in the country, which is a major advantage for recruiting and for fill rates. Two honest caveats shape the plan: it is summer, so a large share of seasonal residents are up north, and it is hurricane season (roughly June through November), which can disrupt scheduling. Like Phoenix, we run this as a base-building launch: prove tables form and fill with year-round residents now, and prime a very strong teacher base for the fall and winter season.

## Targets (30 days)

- Ambassador target: 2 to 3 (a teacher plus an organizer; this market has many teachers to choose from).
- Table target: 7 tables created, 4 filled, 2 recurring.
- Players reached: 15 or more distinct year-round residents.
- Status goal: reach Green by Day 30, where summer Green means at least 1 to 2 recurring tables plus a deep teacher and venue base ready for the season.

## Why these targets

The mahjong density here means tables that do form should fill faster than in most markets, so we set fill and recurring a notch above Phoenix. But the summer exodus and storm risk keep the table-created number modest. The biggest prize this month is the teacher base: signing several teachers now sets up an outsized fall.

## 30-day launch checklist

### Week 0: Setup
- [ ] Confirm the lead ambassador (a well-connected year-round teacher is ideal).
- [ ] Assign referral code, for example FMM-BOCA-{FIRSTNAME}.
- [ ] List 3 indoor venue candidates (JCC, library, clubhouse, community center).
- [ ] Ask the ambassador to flag year-round players versus seasonal ones.

### Week 1: First table and teacher outreach
- [ ] Lead ambassador starts their first table on /start.
- [ ] Invite year-round players to claim seats.
- [ ] Begin the teacher base list (this market is rich with teachers; aim to add several).
- [ ] Milestone: 1 to 2 tables created, 1 filling.

### Week 2: Fill and recruit a second ambassador
- [ ] Fill the first table (4 of 4).
- [ ] Sign a second ambassador (another teacher or an organizer).
- [ ] Confirm 1 indoor venue partner.
- [ ] Add at least 4 teachers or venues to the season base list.
- [ ] Milestone: 3 tables created, 2 filled, first game played.

### Week 3: Recurring and a beginner session
- [ ] Turn a filled table into a recurring game.
- [ ] Run a beginner-friendly session at the indoor venue.
- [ ] Second ambassador has a table filling.
- [ ] Milestone: 5 tables created, 3 filled, 1 recurring.

### Week 4: Two recurring games and a season pipeline
- [ ] Confirm 2 recurring games are holding.
- [ ] Finalize the season surge plan: which teachers and venues host when seasonal residents return, plus a waitlist to contact in the fall.
- [ ] Document what worked.
- [ ] Milestone: 7 tables created, 4 filled, 2 recurring, plus a strong season pipeline. Status Green.

## Risks

1. Summer exodus. Many seasonal residents are up north from late spring into the fall. Chasing them wastes the month.
2. Hurricane season. Storms and warnings (June through November) can cancel a game or a venue on short notice.
3. Heat and humidity. Indoor venues only; outdoor plans fail.
4. Teachers away. Some of the strongest teachers also travel in summer.

## Recovery actions

1. For the exodus: target year-round residents now and build a named fall waitlist of seasonal players to activate when they return. Do not count absent seasonal players toward the summer targets.
2. For storms: keep games indoors at flexible venues, set a simple rule that a storm-week game moves to the next clear day, and lean on recurring games so a single cancellation does not break the habit.
3. For heat and humidity: indoor, air-conditioned venues by default; morning games.
4. For teachers being away: use the market's depth to your advantage by signing two or three teachers, so coverage holds, and keep building the base list every week even when play is light.
