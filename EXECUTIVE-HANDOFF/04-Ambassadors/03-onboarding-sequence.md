# Ambassador Onboarding Sequence

What happens after an applicant is approved. The job of onboarding is one thing: get the new ambassador to their first real table within about 2 weeks, and to a recurring game within about 60 days. Signed is not the goal. Activated is.

## The activation milestone

- Activated = the ambassador has formed at least one real table on the site.
- Filled = that table reached 4 of 4 players.
- Recurring = a table that plays on a standing day and time.

Every touch below is designed to move the ambassador from approved to activated to recurring. Tie your check-ins to these, not to vanity steps.

## Design notes for the founder

- Most ambassadors are warm, capable people who are new to the tool. Many are in their 60s or 70s. Keep every instruction short and plain, and offer to do the first table together on the phone.
- Channel: use email for the materials and the record, use text for nudges and quick wins. Where a touch can be either, pick what that person responds to.
- Tokens: {{ambassador_first_name}}, {{city}}, {{referral_code}}, {{referral_link}}.

## Touch 2. How it works (Day 1)

Subject: How it works, in 4 simple steps

Body:
{{ambassador_first_name}}, here is the whole thing in four steps.

1. Start a table. Go to findmymahjgame.com/start. Pick a day, a time, and your general area (a neighborhood or ZIP, never your home address). Add your first name and a phone or email. That creates a table with four seats, and you take the first seat.
2. Invite your players. Share your link or the table link with the people you already know. They tap Claim a Seat and add their first name and a phone or email. You can see the seats fill up.
3. The site does the coordinating. When the fourth seat fills, the site emails everyone with first names and your general area, and a reminder to meet in a public place for the first game. Replies come to you, the host, so the group can pick a spot.
4. Play, then keep it going. After you play, you can start the same table again for next week. That is how a one-time game becomes a standing game.

That is it. Want to start your first table right now while it is fresh? I will watch for it. If anything is confusing, call me, I mean it.

Shauna

## What the founder tracks during onboarding

For each new ambassador, watch these in the tracking sheet and the metrics dashboard:
- Days from approval to first table (activation speed). Aim for under 14.
- Whether the first table filled.
- Whether a recurring game started by day 60.

If an ambassador has not started a table by Day 7, the Day 7 check-in plus a personal call is the single most important intervention. Do not let a signed ambassador go quiet without a real conversation.

## Handing off to the rest of the system

Once an ambassador is activated, they move into the steady rhythm in the City Launch Checklist (recruit co-hosts, line up a public venue, seed more tables) and they show up on the KPI Dashboard. Onboarding is done when the first table is real and a path to recurring is clear.
