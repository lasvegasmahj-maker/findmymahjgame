## How it works, in plain terms

1. You share your link and the scripts below with your players.
2. A host taps "Start a Table," picks a day, a time, and their general area or ZIP (never an exact address), and adds a first name and a phone or email. That host takes seat 1.
3. Players claim the open seats with a first name and a phone or email. The page shows a live seat counter, like "3 of 4 players, 1 seat to go," and a "Need a 4th" badge when a table is one seat away.
4. When the 4th seat fills, the site emails all four players automatically with first names, the general area, and a friendly nudge to meet in a public place for the first game. The host is the coordinator, so replies go to the host.
5. Contact details are never shared between players. The site handles the introductions.

That is the whole loop. You start the spark; the site does the coordinating.

## 6. FAQ for your players

**Is it free?**
Yes. FindMyMahj is completely free. There are no fees and no membership.

**Is it safe?**
Yes. New groups meet in public places like libraries, community centers, cafes, or senior centers for the first game. The site never shares your phone number or your home address with other players. When you set up a table, you give a general area or ZIP, never an exact address. Introductions are handled by the site, and the host acts as the coordinator.

**Do I need to be good at mahjong?**
No. Beginners and longtime players are both welcome. Many tables enjoy a mix of experience levels, and it is a friendly way to learn or to keep your game sharp.

**Where do we meet?**
Your group chooses a public place for the first game, such as a library, community center, cafe, or senior center. These spots are comfortable, easy to find, and welcoming. After you have played and gotten to know your table, you can decide together where to meet next.

**How do I start or join?**
Use the link from your ambassador. To start a table, tap "Start a Table," pick a day and time and your general area, and add your first name and a phone or email. To join, open a table page and tap "Claim a Seat." You will hear from the site once your table is full.

## 7. Safety talking points

Use these when you talk with players, in person or in writing. They keep everyone comfortable and protect the welcoming feel of the community.

- **First games are in public places.** Always suggest a library, community center, cafe, or senior center for the first time a new group meets. These places are easy, comfortable, and open to all.
- **We never share phone numbers or home addresses.** The site handles introductions. Players see first names and a general area only.
- **No exact addresses are published.** When someone starts a table, they give a general area or ZIP, not their street address.
- **The host is the coordinator.** Replies go to the host, so there is a clear point of contact for each table.
- **Encourage people to bring a friend.** New players often feel more at ease arriving with someone they know, and it helps tables fill faster.
- **Keep it warm and patient.** Many players are settling into a new group. A friendly welcome goes a long way.
