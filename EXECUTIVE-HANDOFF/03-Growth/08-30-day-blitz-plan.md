# 30-Day Ambassador Blitz Plan

One month. 25 Founding Ambassadors. 25 cities. The point of the blitz is not just to sign people, it is to get real tables forming. An ambassador who signs but never starts a table does not count.

## The target

| Metric | 30-day goal |
|--------|-------------|
| Founding Ambassadors signed | 25, one per city |
| Ambassadors activated (started at least 1 table) | 20 |
| Tables formed | 50 or more |
| Tables filled (4 of 4) | 25 or more |
| Recurring games started | 10 or more |

North Star: Tables Formed, then Tables Filled, then Games Played, then Recurring Tables. Every action below exists to move that funnel.

## How to read this plan

- Weekly target is the number you are driving to by Friday of that week.
- Founder actions are Shauna's daily to-do list. Keep each day to about 60 to 90 minutes of focused work. Jason supports on anything technical (referral codes, the admin tab, metrics).
- Do outreach in the morning when your energy is highest. Do onboarding and check-ins in the afternoon.

## Before Day 1 (setup, half a day)

- [ ] Build the master tracking sheet (see the Referral Tracking Plan). One row per city.
- [ ] Lay your Oh My Mahjong contacts over the Top 25 Target Cities list. Mark every warm city.
- [ ] Fill the 25 city slots: warm contacts first, then highest-ranked open cities.
- [ ] Load your recruiting email sequence into your email tool with the tokens ready.
- [ ] Remove the smoke-test record from the ambassadors table.
- [ ] Decide your referral-code format (see the Referral Tracking Plan), for example FMM-LV-SHAUNA.

## Week 1: Foundation and the first 6 (Tier 1 cities)

Weekly target: 6 ambassadors signed, 6 onboarded, first 6 tables started.

Focus cities: Las Vegas/Henderson, Boca Raton/Palm Beach, Naples, Sarasota, Scottsdale/Phoenix, The Villages.

Daily founder actions:

- Monday
  - [ ] Send recruiting Email 1 to your 6 warmest Tier 1 contacts.
  - [ ] Personally text the 2 warmest ("Loved meeting you at Oh My Mahjong, just sent you a note, would love your help").
  - [ ] Start your own first table in Las Vegas on /start so you have a live example to show.
- Tuesday
  - [ ] Send Email 1 to the next batch of Tier 1 contacts.
  - [ ] Reply to every response within the day. Book a 15-minute call with anyone who says yes.
  - [ ] Approve any applications that came in (admin Ambassadors tab).
- Wednesday
  - [ ] Run 2 to 3 onboarding calls. Walk each new ambassador through /start and give them their referral code.
  - [ ] Send the onboarding welcome email to anyone approved.
- Thursday
  - [ ] Send recruiting Email 2 (follow-up) to non-responders.
  - [ ] Help your first ambassadors start their first table. Get on the phone and do it with them if needed.
  - [ ] Check the metrics dashboard. Note tables formed.
- Friday
  - [ ] Onboarding calls for the rest of the Tier 1 group.
  - [ ] Update the tracking sheet. Confirm you have 6 signed and 6 with a table started.
  - [ ] Send a short Friday encouragement note to all active ambassadors ("Here is what we built together this week").
- Weekend (light)
  - [ ] Reply to messages. Send Email 2 to anyone still warm. Rest.

End-of-week check: 6 signed, 6 onboarded, at least 4 tables formed. If you are short on tables, the gap is almost always that ambassadors have not invited their own players yet. Get them to send the launch-kit invite scripts.

## Week 2: Scale to 14 (add Tier 2 cities)

Weekly target: 14 ambassadors total signed, 12 activated, 20 tables formed cumulatively.

Focus cities: Long Island, Westchester, Northern NJ, Los Angeles, San Diego, Palm Springs, Dallas, Houston.

Daily founder actions (repeat the Week 1 rhythm with these adjustments):

- Each morning: send recruiting Email 1 to 3 to 4 new Tier 2 cities, and Email 2 or 3 to last week's non-responders.
- Each afternoon: 2 to 3 onboarding calls, plus 1 check-in call with a Week 1 ambassador to push them toward their first filled table.
- Wednesday: send the onboarding "start your first table this week" challenge to the Week 1 group.
- Friday: update the sheet, confirm 14 signed, celebrate the first filled tables publicly to the group (no last names, no contact info).

End-of-week check: 14 signed, at least 1 table filled, first sign of a recurring game.

## Week 3: Expand to 22 (Tier 3 cities) and turn on recurring

Weekly target: 22 ambassadors total, 18 activated, 35 tables formed cumulatively, first recurring games confirmed.

Focus cities: Boston, Philadelphia, Washington DC, Charlotte, Raleigh/Durham, Hilton Head, Charleston, Denver.

Daily founder actions:

- Morning: recruiting outreach to Tier 3 cities. By now you have proof and testimonials from Weeks 1 and 2; put a one-line ambassador win in every recruiting email.
- Afternoon: shift more time to ambassador success than to recruiting. Run "make it recurring" check-ins with every ambassador who has a filled table. Help them set a standing day and time.
- Mid-week: spotlight a city that is working. Ask that ambassador for a 2-sentence quote you can use in recruiting and onboarding.
- Friday: confirm 22 signed and at least 5 recurring games in motion.

End-of-week check: 22 signed, 35 plus tables formed, 5 plus recurring games.

## Week 4: Finish at 25 and lock in the system

Weekly target: 25 ambassadors total, 20 activated, 50 tables formed cumulatively, 25 filled, 10 recurring.

Daily founder actions:

- Sign the final 3 cities. Use honorable-mention cities if a target city stalled.
- Spend most of the week on activation, not recruiting. Personally help every signed-but-inactive ambassador start their first table.
- Run a 20-minute group call or send a group recap: what we built, the leaderboard, and what is next.
- Build the Month 2 list: which cities to double, which ambassadors to promote to mentors, which to re-engage.

End-of-month review (founder, 1 hour):

- [ ] Fill the KPI dashboard numbers for all 25 cities.
- [ ] Rank ambassadors: Activated, Growing, At risk, Dormant.
- [ ] Identify the 5 strongest. Ask them what worked. Bottle it.
- [ ] Identify the 5 weakest. Decide: coach, replace, or pause.
- [ ] Write next month's targets.

## If you fall behind

- Behind on signings? You are spending too long per contact. Send the sequence, do not wait for perfect replies, move on.
- Signed but no tables? This is the real failure mode. Stop recruiting for a day and get on the phone with each ambassador to start a table with them, live.
- Tables forming but not filling? Push the launch-kit invite scripts. Most ambassadors under-invite their own network.
- Overwhelmed? Cut the weekly target, not the activation work. 15 activated ambassadors beat 25 dormant ones.
