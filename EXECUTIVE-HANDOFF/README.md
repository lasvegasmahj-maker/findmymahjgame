# Find My Mahj Game: Executive Handoff

This folder is the complete operating system for the business. Anyone (a new employee, an advisor, an investor, or a helper) can read it and understand what we built, what is live, the strategy, what remains, and what success looks like, without digging through the codebase.

Start with folder 01.

## Folder guide

- 01-Executive-Briefing. Read this first. The plain-English summary of the whole business, plus the Master Index that maps every document.
- 02-Product. How the product works and what is built.
- 03-Growth. Where we grow (the top 25 cities) and the 30-day plan to do it.
- 04-Ambassadors. The Founding Ambassador program: recruiting, emails, onboarding, the launch kit, and referral tracking.
- 05-City-Launch. The city scorecard and the three proof-market launch plans (Las Vegas, Phoenix, Boca).
- 06-Metrics. How we measure success: the KPI definitions and the one-page weekly dashboard.
- 07-Founder. The decisions only Shauna can make. Answer these to ground the plan.
- 08-Archive. Earlier exploration and technical references, kept for history. Where it disagrees with the current documents, the current documents win.

## The one idea

One ambassador leads to multiple tables, which leads to multiple players, which leads to recurring games. We are in City Launch Mode: prove this in three cities, then expand nationally.

## The North Star

Tables Formed, then Tables Filled, then Games Played, then Recurring Tables. Revenue is not a goal right now. Recurring games are the prize.

## Current status

The product is live. The growth plan is ready. The launch has not started yet. The immediate next step is the founder answering the questions in folder 07 and standing up the Las Vegas launch.
