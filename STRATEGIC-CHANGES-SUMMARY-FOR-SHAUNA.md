# Strategic Changes Summary for Shauna

Hi Shauna,

Read this first. It is the plain-English version of everything. No tech, no jargon, just what is going on and what we are doing about it.

## What changed

For a while, we thought Find My Mahj Game was a "table-formation website." The whole point was helping four people find each other and sit down to play. That is a great feature, and it is live, and people love it. But it was too small a definition of the business.

Here is the new definition, and this is the real reset: Find My Mahj Game is going to become the place every American Mahjong player checks first for anything mahjong. Players. Games. Open plays. Tournaments. Retreats. Cruises. Teachers. Friendly venues. Local groups. Where to play while traveling. Mahjong news. When a player wants any of it, the first thought in their head should be "check Find My Mahj Game."

We call that being the "source of truth." Think of it the way people say "Google it" or "check Zillow for houses." We want "check Find My Mahj Game" to become the habit for mahjong.

Table formation is still in there. It is one of the best features we have. But it is a feature, not the business. The business is being the one place that knows where all the mahjong is happening in America.

## Why it changed

Three reasons.

First, the market is small, and small is good for us. American Mahjong is a tight niche. Being the first and most complete list of events, teachers, and venues is actually a real advantage in a niche this size, because there is not room for five of these. There is room for one. We want to be the one, and we want to get there before anyone else thinks to try.

Second, we already have the relationships, and relationships are how you fill a list like this fast. We have the Las Vegas email list. We have a real CRM with people, sessions, organizations, and instructors already in it. We have warm connections from the Oh My Mahjong conference, which means real teachers, organizers, and studio owners who already know us. We have a reputation for safety and trust with older players, which is gold and very hard to copy. And you are a certified instructor, which gives us instant credibility. Most competitors would have to build all of that from scratch. We already have it.

Third, speed. If a competitor shows up, the thing that protects us is not fancier software. It is having already gathered the events, already signed up the teachers, already earned the trust. You cannot copy a head start built on relationships. You can copy a button on a website.

## What we learned

The old strategy docs said: go deep local first, recurring tables are the whole game, the national hub is far away, and a big directory of everything is "dangerous and shallow."

We were half right. Here is the honest update.

Recurring tables really are the stickiest thing we do. Once four people play together every week, they stay forever. That is the deepest habit and the hardest to lose. We keep that. We do not abandon Las Vegas.

But the part where we said "the national hub is far off" was too cautious. In a niche this small, being first and most complete on events, teachers, and venues is itself a moat, and we can build it fast through the relationships we already have, by gathering events and contacts that are already public. That is not shallow. That is a land grab.

So we are doing both at once. Gather the inventory nationally (events, teachers, venues) because that is the fast land grab and the clear path to being the source of truth. And seed real recurring tables in Las Vegas because that is the deepest habit moat. The newsletter ties it all together by giving us a way to reach players every single week and build the "check Find My Mahj Game" habit.

The thing we were wrong about: waiting. Waiting is the one luxury we do not have.

## What matters most in the next 30 days

Five things, in plain order of importance.

1. Gather inventory. Every event, open play, tournament, retreat, cruise, teacher, and friendly venue you can find that is already public goes into our list. This is the land grab. This is what makes us the source of truth. Quantity and being first matter more than being polished right now.

2. Launch the weekly newsletter. This is how we reach players every week and become a habit. It does not need to be fancy. It needs to go out, every week, on time, starting now. The Las Vegas email list is the seed audience.

3. Recruit Founding Ambassadors. These are local champions who feed us events and players from their own areas. Each good one is a person who fills our list in a city we cannot personally reach. They multiply us.

4. Teacher outreach. Teachers are the center of every local mahjong scene. If a teacher is on our side, their students come with them. Warm conversations, not cold pitches. We already know many of them.

5. Seed Las Vegas recurring tables. Keep our home base strong. Get real groups playing on a real schedule. This is the proof that the deep version works, and it is your backyard.

Notice what is NOT on this list: building more software. The site does enough right now. The next 30 days are about people, relationships, and gathering what already exists out in the world, not about adding features.

## What success looks like by Day 30

If we win the next month, here is what is true on Day 30:

- We have a real, growing list of mahjong events, teachers, and venues across multiple states. Not a handful. A list that already makes us the most complete one out there.
- The newsletter has gone out every week, on schedule, with a real and growing readership.
- We have a first group of Founding Ambassadors who are actively sending us events and players from their areas.
- A meaningful number of teachers know us, like us, and are feeding us their information.
- Las Vegas has live recurring tables that play on a schedule, proving the deep model.
- And the most important soft signal: a few players, somewhere, have said out loud "oh, just check Find My Mahj Game." When that phrase starts showing up on its own, we are winning.

We will not be finished in 30 days. Source of truth is a long game. But in 30 days we can be far enough ahead that catching us becomes very hard. That is the whole goal. Get a head start that relationships protect and speed builds, before anyone else realizes this niche is worth taking.

One last thing. Do not let the size of the vision scare you. You do not build a source of truth in a day. You build it one event, one teacher, one ambassador, one newsletter at a time. Do that every day for 30 days and you will be amazed where you stand.
