# FindMyMahj War Room: 30/60-Day Blitz

**Primary metric: Active Tables Formed** (4 real people, scheduled, who actually play). Everything else is a leading indicator.

## The one assumption I'm challenging up front
The war-room objectives (25 cities + 50 ambassadors + 1,000 users + 500 emails in 30 days) mix **depth** and **breadth** goals that fight each other. Going 25 cities wide in 30 days yields 25 thin, empty pages and near-zero real tables, the cold-start death the product-model review already flagged. **A funded competitor does not beat you to "25 cities with a directory"; they beat you to liquidity. So we win the same way they can't fake: concentrate force to make a few metros genuinely liquid (real tables that recur), and let ambassadors + loops + SEO carry breadth.** Reordered objectives:

1. **Form 25 real tables** (the metric) — concentrate in ~5 metros, not 25.
2. **Recruit ambassadors who each bring instant liquidity** — quality over the number 50; 15 activated teachers who each seed a table beats 50 names who seed nothing.
3. **1,000 users / 500 emails** — an output of the loops + ambassador audiences, not a separate push.
4. **"Own" cities** = a city has recurring real tables, not a published page. Own 5 truly; seed the next 20 for SEO without claiming they're active.

Depth first creates the proof and the testimonials that then make breadth cheap. That is the land grab.

## Workstream A: Product (ranked by impact on Active Tables Formed)
Build only what moves the metric. Ranked:
1. **Fill-time coordination email** (just built) — without it tables form but never play. #1 by definition.
2. **Start a Table / Claim / Share / I Want to Play** (built) — the engine.
3. **"Need a 4th" urgency** — a table at 3/4 gets a time-boxed "1 seat to go" push to nearby "I want to play" signups + a re-share nudge. Directly converts forming → full.
4. **Recurring tables** — one tap to "make this our weekly game," which turns one formed table into N games (and is the retention moat). Highest leverage after the basics.
5. **SMS** (confirmations + the 4th-seat alert) — this audience acts on texts, not email. Biggest conversion upgrade.
6. **"View My Tables" by phone** — return path, no account.
Defer: profiles, reviews, chat, native app, anything else.

## Workstream B: Supply (fastest path to 1,000 real listings)
Order = fastest real tables first (detail in [60-day/02-supply-acquisition.md](60-day/02-supply-acquisition.md)):
1. **Teachers first (ambassadors).** A teacher arrives with 8-40 students = instant tables. Peer outreach from Shauna (certified instructor). This is the fastest path, full stop.
2. **Existing organizers + clubs** (Facebook group admins, league runners): they already hold rosters; recruit, don't rebuild.
3. **Legally-clean scraping:** LibCal (libraries), JCC/community-center public class pages, Eventbrite mahjong, parks-and-rec, senior centers. ~3,600 libraries + 170+ JCCs, attributed + back-linked. Mechanical, runs in parallel.
4. **Retirement / 55+ communities** (Sun City, The Villages, Del Webb): disproportionate mahjong density; activities offices are a warm channel.
Outreach + ambassador + referral systems: templates and the program are in [60-day/01-ambassador-program.md](60-day/01-ambassador-program.md). Fastest path to 1,000 listings = teacher rosters + LibCal/JCC scrape running together.

## Workstream D: Competitive intelligence (summary; full in [01-competitive-intelligence.md](01-competitive-intelligence.md))
- **Bam Good Time** (~1,200 clubs, strongest SEO): owns clubs, **has no individual-player / find-a-4th directory** (confirmed). Out-execute on the player/table layer + freshness + claimability they don't have.
- **NMJL**: owns the card and the audience, ignores discovery entirely. No threat in our lane; a future partner.
- **Oh My Mahjong**: product brand, discovery is a side feature to sell tiles. Partner, not competitor.
- **Facebook Groups**: where demand lives but ephemeral and invisible to Google. We make that demand persistent + searchable. Don't fight head-on; harvest via admins.
- **Likely future entrant**: a funded events/social app. **Our defense = the table/player graph + ambassador relationships + recurring-game habit**, which take time and trust to build and can't be bought fast. Move now.

## Workstream E: Network-effect loops (strongest first)
1. **Table-forming loop:** Start a Table → share to friends + FB group → seats fill → each new player can start/ą join another → more tables. (Built; the share + seat counter drive it.)
2. **Need-a-4th loop:** a forming table pulls in "I want to play" signups; filling it creates a happy testimonial that recruits the next host.
3. **Ambassador loop:** teacher seeds tables → students become players → some become hosts/ambassadors in adjacent areas.
4. **SEO ↔ supply loop:** each real table mints an indexable city page → searchers arrive → more tables (long-horizon).
Instrument the K-factor: invites sent per host × invite→join rate. Push it toward 1.

## Workstream F: Retention (return weekly; one-off → recurring)
- **Recurring tables**: one tap to "make this weekly" (the single biggest retention lever; build it, Workstream A #4).
- **Weekly "your game is in 2 days" reminder** tied to the fixed slot, the habit cue.
- **Easiest backfill in the world**: when a regular can't make it, one tap posts "need a sub this week," and the area's player pool fills it. This is the genuinely sticky utility (better than any competitor) and the reason a formed group stays on-platform instead of moving to a group text.
- **Seasonal**: the spring NMJL card release = an annual re-engagement spike; build the list all year.
