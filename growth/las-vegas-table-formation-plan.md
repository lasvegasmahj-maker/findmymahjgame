# Las Vegas Table Formation Plan

**Goal:** manually create the first **10 recurring mahjong tables** in the Las Vegas valley over the next 30 days. Las Vegas is the proof city. Every table here becomes the template, and the script, every Founding Ambassador will copy in their own city.

**Definition of a "formed table":** 4 confirmed players, a fixed day/time/place, that has played **3 weeks in a row.** Not a listing. Not an RSVP. Four people, three weeks.

**The method is concierge, not software.** Shauna forms each table by hand (text, call, in person), then lists it on FindMyMahj via the new **Start a Table** flow so it is real, shareable, and self-fills the inevitable dropouts. The software exists to support these tables, not the other way around.

**Why Shauna can do this fast:** she is a certified instructor with a roster of current and former students, she runs MAHJ101/102 classes, and she is known in the Las Vegas mahjong Facebook groups. That is a warm list of dozens of people who already want a game. Tables 1-3 come straight off that list this week.

## The per-table playbook (repeat 10 times)

1. **Pull the list.** From Shauna's students + the FB group + "I want to play" signups, find 6-8 people who match the table's area and day (you over-recruit because not everyone commits).
2. **Send the concierge text** (below). Lock the first 4 who say yes.
3. **Book the slot.** Confirm the venue (call the library/community center for a recurring room, or a member's home for invite-only).
4. **List it on FindMyMahj** using **Start a Table** (`/start`). Now it is a real, shareable page with a seats counter, and it backfills itself when someone drops.
5. **Play week 1.** Shauna or the host attends the first session to make it warm.
6. **Hand it off.** Name a permanent host from the four. Shauna steps back.
7. **Keep it full.** When someone drops, the table's share link + "Need a 4th" fills the seat without Shauna.

For the FB group:

## Weekly cadence (Shauna's 30 minutes a day)

- **Mon:** lock this week's new table (4 confirmed). Update the dashboard: tables formed.
- **Tue-Thu:** attend/seed a session; recruit for next week's table; answer "I want to play" signups by texting them into the nearest forming table.
- **Fri:** fill check, any table short a 4th gets a share nudge.
- **Sun:** plan next week's table + which ambassador to recruit.

## 30-day target
**10 tables formed (4 players, playing).** 6-8 of them surviving to 3 consecutive weeks = the real win. Plus: 3-4 of the hosts recruited as the first Founding Ambassadors, and every table live on FindMyMahj as the first real listings, dogfooding the wedge before any other city.

## What makes a table "stick" (so we don't just form and lose them)
- A **named permanent host** (not Shauna) by week 2.
- A **fixed recurring slot** (same day/time/place) so it becomes a habit, the "Tuesday game."
- The **share link** so dropouts backfill without anyone's inbox.
- A **15-minute Monday check** on which tables are short, so none quietly dies.
