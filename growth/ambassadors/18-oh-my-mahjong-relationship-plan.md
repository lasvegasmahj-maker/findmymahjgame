# Oh My Mahjong Relationship Plan

The Oh My Mahjong relationships are the single highest-leverage growth asset in the business right now. This plan turns warm conference contacts into activated ambassadors who form recurring tables. Track every contact in the Warm Relationship Pipeline dashboard (the founder KPI).

## Relationship prioritization: Hot, Warm, Cold

Sort every Oh My Mahjong contact into one of three buckets. The bucket decides how fast and how personally you move.

Hot (move this week):
- You have a genuine personal rapport, they expressed interest in helping or in the mission, and they can convene players now (a teacher with students, a club organizer, a studio owner).
- Action: a personal call or text within days. Aim to sign and start their first table together on that call.

Warm (move within two weeks):
- A real but lighter connection. They were friendly, they fit the profile, but interest in helping is unconfirmed.
- Action: the warm recruiting email sequence, then a call if they respond. Nurture, do not push.

Cold (low priority, keep on the list):
- A name and a card but little real connection, or unclear fit.
- Action: a single warm note that references the conference. If no response, leave them; do not spend founder time chasing.

Rule: a contact's heat can change. A Warm contact who replies eagerly becomes Hot. Re-sort as you learn.

## City prioritization framework

Overlay contacts on the city plan. A contact's value rises sharply if they are in a proof or launch city.

1. Proof and launch cities first: Las Vegas, Scottsdale/Phoenix, Boca/Palm Beach. A Hot contact here is the top of the entire list.
2. Then Top 25 cities where you have a Hot or Warm contact.
3. Then strong Hot contacts in any city (a great person in an unplanned city can open that city).
4. Deprioritize cities where you have only Cold contacts and no plan.

The scoring rule of thumb: priority = heat times city-fit times reach. A Hot, high-reach contact in Las Vegas outranks everything. A Cold contact in an unplanned city is bottom.

## Recruiting strategy

- Lead with the relationship, not the product. Reference the conference and something specific you remember.
- Make the ask small and concrete: help start mahjong in their city, free, with tools that do the coordinating, starting with one table.
- Offer the Founding Ambassador package, with the real reward (priority referrals, support) earned after the first table.
- Do the first table with them, live, on the recruiting call when possible. Activation on the first call is the goal.
- Log everyone in the pipeline dashboard and advance them stage by stage.

## Outreach cadence

- Hot: personal call or text now, then a same-week follow-up to start the table. Weekly touch until activated.
- Warm: Day 0, 3, 7, 12 sequence (the recruiting emails), then a call on any reply. If silent after Day 12, move to a monthly check-in.
- Cold: one note now, one optional follow-up in a month, then leave.
- Across all: never let an Approved-but-not-Activated contact go more than a week without a personal nudge. That gap is where the program leaks.

## Launch sequence

1. Build the list: enter every Oh My Mahjong contact into the pipeline, tagged Hot/Warm/Cold, with city and role.
2. Work the Hot list in proof and launch cities first. Sign and activate.
3. As each signs, run the onboarding sequence to a first table within two weeks.
4. Fill tables using their own networks plus our priority referrals.
5. Convert filled tables into recurring games.
6. Then work Warm contacts in priority cities, then Hot contacts in other cities.
7. Review weekly on the Warm Relationship Pipeline and the City Launch Scorecard; double down on producers, re-engage or release the dormant.

## Where founder time should go

Founder time is the scarcest resource. Spend it where it converts.

- Most of it on Hot contacts in proof and launch cities: the calls that sign an ambassador and start a table on the spot.
- A meaningful slice on activating the already-signed: getting Approved ambassadors to their first filled table. This is higher value than recruiting more names, because a signed-but-dormant ambassador is wasted potential.
- A little on Warm nurture in priority cities.
- Almost none on Cold contacts or on cities with no plan. A single note, then move on.
- The test before any founder hour: will this hour move a contact one stage toward Activated, or move a table toward filled and recurring. If not, it is the wrong hour.
