# Founder-Assisted vs Organic Growth Dashboard

The single most important measure of whether the model works. Early on, Shauna will personally make tables happen. That proves demand, not scalability. The business is only real when tables form without her. This dashboard tracks that shift and makes it the goal.

## The definitions (keep them strict)

- Founder-Assisted table: Shauna personally helped create or fill it. She recruited the host, made the calls, sat the players, or hosted it herself. If her direct effort was needed for it to exist, it is Founder-Assisted.
- Organic table: created and filled without Shauna's direct involvement. An ambassador, a host, or a player started it and filled it using the tool, the referral links, and our automatic systems. Shauna may have trained the ambassador months ago; that is fine. If she did not touch this specific table, it is Organic.

Edge rule: when in doubt, call it Founder-Assisted. We want to under-claim Organic, not over-claim it, so the metric stays honest.

## The one number that matters: Organic Ratio

Organic Ratio = Organic tables / all tables, measured on recurring tables.

- Month 1: expect a low ratio (most tables are Founder-Assisted). That is normal and fine.
- The goal is the ratio rising over time. A flat or falling ratio means we are scaling Shauna's calendar, not the business.
- Target trajectory: Month 1 mostly assisted; by Month 3, at least half of new recurring tables Organic; by Month 6, the large majority Organic.

## Weekly scorecard

Fill every Friday. Track recurring tables (the real asset), with formed and filled as leading indicators.

| Week | Tables formed (A / O) | Tables filled (A / O) | Recurring (A / O) | Organic ratio (recurring) | Notes |
|------|----------------------|----------------------|-------------------|---------------------------|-------|

A = Founder-Assisted, O = Organic. Compute the Organic ratio on the recurring column.

The only acceptable trend is the Organic ratio going up. If it is not, the action is not "recruit more"; it is "find why hosts cannot run tables without Shauna" and fix that (better onboarding, better tools, better co-hosts).

## How to record it

- Tag each table at creation as Assisted or Organic in your tracking sheet (and, once the schema is approved, a simple flag on the table record).
- The attribution rollup in /admin/metrics already shows tables by referral code; an ambassador-coded table that Shauna did not touch is Organic. Cross-check there.
- Review alongside the City Launch Scorecard and the Warm Relationship Pipeline every Friday.

## What good and bad look like

- Healthy: total recurring tables rising AND the Organic share rising. The flywheel is turning.
- Warning: total recurring rising but Organic flat. You are the engine; that does not scale. Shift effort from making tables to enabling hosts.
- Failure: by day 60, zero Organic recurring tables. The model is not proven; rethink onboarding, host selection, or the product friction that stops a host from running a table alone.

## Why this is the right north for the next 30 to 90 days

Tables formed, filled, and played measure activity. The Organic ratio measures whether that activity can outlive the founder's personal effort. That is the difference between a business and a very busy hobby. Protect this metric; it tells the truth.
