# Find My Mahj Game: 30-Day Founder Calendar

**For Shauna. Las Vegas, first market. Wartime tempo.**

Goal: turn the Las Vegas Mahjong email list and warm Oh My Mahjong relationships into recurring tables, a live weekly newsletter, a stocked directory of events, teachers, and venues, and a bench of activated Founding Ambassadors. Relationships and the newsletter come first.

---

## How to use this calendar

- Plan for about 3-4 focused hours on weekdays. Weekends are lighter (about 1-2 hours, mostly replies and logging).
- **Mondays** are the week's outreach push (you send the most asks on Monday).
- **Fridays** are the scorecard review (you measure, log, and plan next week).
- **Every Wednesday** is newsletter day: Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly goes out.
- Log everything in Airtable as you go (People, Sessions, Attendance, Corporate Leads, Organizations, Instructors). If it is not in Airtable, it did not happen.
- Product surfaces for every ask: findmymahjgame.com/start, /play, /t/CODE, /ambassadors, and hello@findmymahjgame.com.
- Safety, always: public venues only, first names only in public, never publish a home address or personal phone number.

**The Las Vegas geography you are working:** Summerlin, Henderson and Green Valley, Anthem and Sun City, Centennial Hills, Spring Valley, and central Las Vegas.

# WEEK 1: Mine the list, light the first fires, launch the newsletter

The theme of Week 1 is relationships and the first newsletter. You will read the whole list, sort it, and personally reach out to your warmest people. By Wednesday the newsletter is live.

### Day 1 ,  Monday: Mine the Las Vegas list
- Export the full Las Vegas Mahjong email list and load every person into Airtable **People** if not already there. Add a tag column: `Warm`, `Player`, `Curious`, `Possible Ambassador`, `Possible Teacher`.
- Read every contact and tag them. Star your top 25 warmest people (past students, repeat repliers, helpers). These are your Week 1 calls and your ambassador pool.
- Draft your "neighborhood" segmentation: add a `Neighborhood` field and fill it where you know it (Summerlin, Henderson, etc.). Where you do not know, that is your first newsletter ask.

### Day 2 ,  Tuesday: Personal notes to your warmest 25
- Send 10-12 short, personal one-to-one emails to your warmest people. Not a blast. Real notes. Use this template:

> Subject: A quick hello (and something new I'm building)
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> It's Shauna. I've been quietly building something for our Las Vegas mahjong community and I wanted you to be one of the first to know.
>
> It's called Find My Mahj Game. The idea is simple: make it easy to find a friendly game near you, and easy to start one if you can't.
>
> Before I tell the whole list, I'd love to know two things. What neighborhood are you in, and would you want a regular game close to home? Just reply, even one line is perfect.
>
> Warmly,
> Shauna

- Log every send in Airtable. As replies come in, note neighborhood and interest.

### Day 3 ,  Wednesday: Launch Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly (Issue 1)
- Write and send **Issue 1**. Keep it warm, short, and useful. The single goal of Issue 1 is to reintroduce yourself, announce Find My Mahj Game, and collect neighborhoods.

> **Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly ,  Issue 1**
>
> Hi friends,
>
> Welcome to the very first Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly. If we've shared a table or a lesson, you know me. If we haven't yet, I'm Shauna, a certified instructor here in the valley, and I love this game.
>
> Here's what's new. I've built Find My Mahj Game to do two simple things: help you **find a friendly game near you**, and help you **start one** when there isn't one yet.
>
> **This week's one small ask:** reply to this email with your neighborhood (Summerlin, Henderson, Centennial Hills, anywhere). I'm mapping where our players are so I can help connect you with games close to home.
>
> Want to get going now?
> - Find a game: findmymahjgame.com/play
> - Start a table: findmymahjgame.com/start
>
> A little mahjong joy for your week: in American Mahjong the Jokers are wild, and they're ours alone. No other version of the game has them.
>
> See you at the table,
> Shauna
> hello@findmymahjgame.com

- After sending, set up a simple reply-tracking view in Airtable to capture neighborhoods.

### Day 4 ,  Thursday: Teacher blitz (warm relationships first)
- Reach out one-to-one to 6-8 teachers and instructors you know from Oh My Mahjong. Ask two things: would they be listed in the directory, and do they have a class or game you can list. Template:

> Subject: Listing you in Find My Mahj Game
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> So good to see you at Oh My Mahjong. I'm building Find My Mahj Game, a simple place for players to find teachers, games, and events. I'd love to list you.
>
> Could I add your name (first name in public for safety), your teaching area, and any class or game you want players to find? If you have a session coming up, I'll list it too.
>
> Reply with whatever you're comfortable sharing and I'll handle the rest.
>
> Thank you,
> Shauna

- Log each teacher in **Instructors**. List anyone who says yes immediately.

### Day 5 ,  Friday: First scorecard review
- Fill in the scorecard for Week 1. Count: emails sent, replies, neighborhoods collected, teachers listed, newsletter sent (yes).
- Review replies and pull out 5-8 people who sound like **possible ambassadors** (helpful, social, enthusiastic, well-connected). These are next week's ambassador asks.
- Write down the single biggest bottleneck and one fix for Week 2.

### Day 6 ,  Saturday: Light replies and logging
- Answer every reply that came in this week. Keep notes warm and personal.
- Make sure every neighborhood answer is logged. Tidy the People table.

### Day 7 ,  Sunday: Light planning
- Draft your Week 2 ambassador shortlist and the three neighborhoods with the most interest. Those neighborhoods get your first tables.

# WEEK 2: Recruit ambassadors, stand up the first tables

The theme of Week 2 is people who multiply you. You recruit Founding Ambassadors and convert the warmest interest into your first one or two recurring tables.

### Day 8 ,  Monday: Ambassador recruiting push
- Send personal invitations to your 5-8 shortlisted people to become **Founding Ambassadors**. This is an honor, not a chore. Template:

> Subject: Would you be a Founding Ambassador?
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Your love for this game shines, and I want to ask you something special. Would you be one of our **Founding Ambassadors** for Las Vegas mahjong?
>
> It's simple and warm: you help welcome new players and help a regular game get going in your neighborhood. I give you everything you need and I do the heavy lifting. You'd be one of just a handful of founders shaping this from the start.
>
> If you're curious, here's the page: findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors. Or just reply yes and I'll call you.
>
> With gratitude,
> Shauna

- Log responses in People with the `Ambassador` status.

### Day 9 ,  Tuesday: Stand up Table #1
- Pick the neighborhood with the most interest. Email the 4-6 interested players there and propose a specific first game at a **public venue** (a library, community center, or cafe with table space).

> Subject: A regular game in {{city}}, want in?
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> A few of us in {{city}} want a regular, friendly mahjong game and I'd love for you to be one of the first at the table.
>
> I'm proposing {{day}} mornings at a public spot nearby (a library or community center, easy parking, welcoming). All levels welcome, and I'll make sure beginners feel at home.
>
> Claim your seat here: findmymahjgame.com/t/CODE
>
> Reply with the days that work and I'll lock it in.
>
> Warmly,
> Shauna

- Create the table in **Sessions** and generate its /t/CODE. List it on /play.

### Day 10 ,  Wednesday: Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly (Issue 2)
- Send **Issue 2**. Now you have something to show: neighborhoods mapped, the first table forming, teachers listed.

### Day 11 ,  Thursday: Venue blitz
- Contact 6-8 **public venues** across two or three neighborhoods: library branches, community centers, senior centers, and cafes with table space. Ask whether they host or would welcome a weekly mahjong group, and what space is available. Template:

> Subject: A welcoming mahjong group at {{venue}}?
>
> Hello,
>
> I'm Shauna, a certified mahjong instructor in Las Vegas. I help friendly, all-ages-welcome mahjong groups meet in good public spaces around the valley.
>
> Does {{venue}} have a table or room where a small weekly group could play? Our players are warm, tidy, and wonderful regulars. I'd love to talk about a standing day and time.
>
> Thank you,
> Shauna
> hello@findmymahjgame.com

- Log each venue in **Organizations**. List confirmed venues on the site.

### Day 12 ,  Friday: Scorecard review
- Update the scorecard. Count ambassadors who said yes, the first table's claimed seats, venues contacted and confirmed, teachers listed.
- Confirm Table #1's day, time, and venue. Make sure it is live on /play with a working /t/CODE.
- Note the bottleneck and the Week 3 fix.

### Day 13 ,  Saturday: Light replies and logging
- Reply to ambassador and venue responses. Onboard any ambassador who said yes with a short, warm welcome and one first task (for example: "help me fill the {{city}} table, here's the seat link").

### Day 14 ,  Sunday: Light planning
- Choose the next two neighborhoods for tables. Draft the player lists for each.

# WEEK 3: Event blitz, second and third tables, activate ambassadors

The theme of Week 3 is inventory and momentum. You become the source of truth for what's happening by listing events, and you stand up tables 2 and 3 with ambassador help.

### Day 15 ,  Monday: Event blitz (become the source of truth)
- Hunt down every American Mahjong event, class, open game, and meetup in the valley over the next 60 days. Sources: senior center calendars, library event pages, community center boards, your teacher network, Oh My Mahjong contacts, and ambassadors.
- List 10-15 events in Airtable and on the site. This is the inventory that makes you the destination.
- Ask each ambassador to send you one event you don't know about yet.

### Day 16 ,  Tuesday: Stand up Table #2 (ambassador-led)
- Hand an ambassador the second neighborhood. Help them email the interested players and propose a specific public-venue game.
- Create Table #2 in **Sessions**, generate its /t/CODE, list it on /play. The ambassador is the warm local host; you are support.

### Day 17 ,  Wednesday: Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly (Issue 3)
- Send **Issue 3**. This issue leans on your new event inventory and the growing table count.

> **Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly ,  Issue 3**

> Hi friends,

> The valley is buzzing. Here's what's happening in Las Vegas mahjong this month.
>
> **Games and events near you:** classes, open games, and meetups are now listed. Find one at findmymahjgame.com/play.

> **Our tables are growing:** we now have regular games forming in {{city}} and {{city}}. Want one near you? Reply with your neighborhood and I'll get to work, or start your own in two minutes at findmymahjgame.com/start.

### Day 18 ,  Thursday: Stand up Table #3 + venue follow-ups

### Day 19 ,  Friday: Scorecard review
- Update the scorecard. You should now have 2-3 tables live, 15+ events listed, several venues confirmed, and ambassadors actively helping.
- Check each table's seat claims via /t/CODE. Any table under 4 confirmed players gets a targeted push next week.
- Note the bottleneck and the Week 4 fix.

### Day 20 ,  Saturday: Light replies and logging
- Reply to event submissions and player questions. Make sure every new event, venue, and table is logged and live.

### Day 21 ,  Sunday: Light planning
- Plan Week 4: which two neighborhoods get tables 4 and 5, and which ambassadors lead them.
