# Find My Mahj Game: Teacher Acquisition Blitz

A wartime founder's toolkit for signing trusted American Mahjong teachers, fast. Teachers are the highest-leverage relationship we have. One teacher brings a roster of students who already trust her, already know the game, and are already looking for tables. We win teachers first, students follow, tables fill, and we become the place every player checks.

This is the 30-day plan. Copy, paste, send.

---

## Part 1: Top Targets and How to Prioritize

### The one-line strategy
Pursue people who already have students. A teacher with 12 students is worth more than 12 individual signups, because she does the trusting for them. Start with the warmest relationships and the people who serve our launch market, then widen out.

### Priority tiers

**Tier 1: Warm Oh My Mahjong conference relationships (contact this week).**
These are the teachers, organizers, and studio owners Shauna already met. Warm relationship plus credibility plus a real roster. The conversion cost is a friendly note, not a cold pitch. Do these first because they cost the least and pay the most.

**Tier 2: Las Vegas local teachers (contact this week).**
Anyone teaching in Summerlin, Henderson and Green Valley, Anthem and Sun City, Centennial Hills, Spring Valley, or central Las Vegas. These directly feed our first market. A Henderson teacher with a Tuesday class is exactly the inventory we are trying to own. Find them through senior centers, community centers, library event calendars, the Jewish Community Center, Active Adult communities (Sun City Summerlin, Sun City Anthem, Del Webb), and parks-and-rec class listings.

**Tier 3: The existing Airtable Instructors table (contact this week, in parallel).**
We already have an Instructors table in the CRM (People, Sessions, Attendance, and Instructors all live there). Anyone already in it is the warmest audience of all. Pull the list, sort by Active Status and most recent Sessions, and reach out first to the ones who have taught with us before.

**Tier 4: NMJL-credentialed instructors and Oh My Mahjong teacher network beyond Vegas (contact next, weeks 2-4).**
Teachers certified to teach the National Mah Jongg League card, and the broader teacher network, especially any in or near future launch cities. They give us national credibility (the source-of-truth goal) and seed future markets. They are a slightly colder ask, so they come after we have a few testimonials in hand.

**Tier 5: Adjacent teachers (ongoing).**
Bridge teachers, canasta and game-day hosts, senior-living activity directors, and rec-center class leaders who could add American Mahjong or refer it. Lower priority, but cheap to keep warm.

### How to rank within a tier
Score each prospect 1-3 on three things and sort by total:
- **Warmth:** Do we already know them? (3 = personal relationship, 2 = one introduction or shared event, 1 = cold.)
- **Roster:** Do they have active students right now? (3 = a current class, 2 = past classes, 1 = unknown.)
- **Geography:** Are they in Las Vegas or a launch city? (3 = Las Vegas, 2 = launch city, 1 = elsewhere.)

Work top scores down. A 9 gets a same-day personal note from Shauna. Anything 5 and below waits until the warm list is worked.

## Part 3: Ambassador Crossover

### Why teachers make the best Founding Ambassadors
A Founding Ambassador grows the game in her area and is recognized for it. Teachers are already doing the job. She already has the trust, the roster, the local knowledge, and the credibility. She is the person other players believe. Asking a teacher to be an ambassador is not adding work, it is naming what she already is and giving her tools and recognition for it.

### When to make the ask
Not in the first message. Lead with the teacher listing (low commitment, all upside). Once she has said "I am in" and forwarded one student link, she has already acted like an ambassador. That is the moment to invite her up.

### The crossover message (send after a teacher is listed and has shared one student link)

**Email subject:** I would love to make you a Founding Ambassador

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> You have already been doing the most important thing: helping players in {{city}} find the game. I would love to make it official and invite you to be one of our Founding Ambassadors.
>
> Founding Ambassadors are the people who grow Mahjong in their area, and we recognize them for it. As a teacher, you are the natural fit. It means:
> - A Founding Ambassador badge on your teacher listing, so players know you are a trusted leader here.
> - Priority when we refer new players and new groups in your neighborhoods.
> - A say in how we grow in {{city}}, because you know it better than we do.
>
> There is no cost and no quota. You keep teaching the way you teach. When you help a new group get started or send a player our way, we recognize it and send more your way.
>
> Want me to set up your Founding Ambassador status? Just say the word and you can learn more here: findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors.

> Warmly,

## Part 4: Referral Incentives (non-cash first, activation-gated)

Reward real activity, never signup. A teacher earns things by helping players, not by saying yes. This keeps the program honest and keeps our listings trustworthy. Everything below is non-cash and tasteful.

### The ladder

| Tier | What the teacher does (the gate) | What she earns | When it unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Listed** | Confirms she teaches, agrees to be listed, shares one student link | Trusted teacher listing on Find My Mahj Game (first name, neighborhoods, what she teaches) | At verification |
| **Active** | 3 of her students find or claim a game through us, or she helps start 1 new group | Featured placement in her area (she appears first when local players search for a teacher), plus a thank-you shout-out in the newsletter | When the 3rd student or 1st group is confirmed active |
| **Founding Ambassador** | Helps start 2+ new recurring groups, or refers 10+ active players over time | Founding Ambassador badge, priority referrals of all new players and groups in her neighborhoods, named recognition as a community leader | When the 2nd group is running or the 10th active player is confirmed |
| **Pillar** | Sustained activity: a group she helped start is still meeting after 60 days, or she has referred 25+ active players | Top-of-area featured status, a spotlight feature (her story, with her permission), and first call on any corporate or group event in her city | At the 60-day group milestone or 25 active players |

### Rules that keep it honest
- **Activity, not signup.** Nothing unlocks because a teacher joined or because a student merely created an account. It unlocks when a player actually finds, claims, or keeps a game, or a group actually meets. "Active" means a real table, a real seat claimed, or a confirmed attendance, the same way Attendance is logged in the CRM.
- **Recognition over reward.** The most valuable thing we give is visibility and priority referrals, not prizes. That is what teachers actually want, and it compounds for us.
- **No pay-to-play.** Featured placement is earned by helping players, never bought. This protects the trust that makes us the source of truth.
- **Tasteful and quiet.** We tell a teacher what she has earned warmly and privately. No leaderboards, no pressure, no public ranking of teachers against each other.

### The "you earned it" note (send when a teacher hits the Active gate)

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> A quick happy note: three of your students have found games through Find My Mahj Game. That is exactly the problem we set out to solve, and you made it happen.
>
> Because of it, you are now featured first when players in {{city}} look for a teacher, and I would love to give you a thank-you shout-out in our next newsletter (only with your okay). You earned it the right way, by helping people play.
>
> Thank you for being one of the good ones.
>
> Warmly,
> Shauna

## Part 5: The Approval Process (vetting and adding a teacher)

We are building the trusted national source of truth. That only works if every listed teacher is real, currently teaching, and safe. Here is the bar and the workflow. The admin teacher workflow lives behind login in the platform, and the Instructors table in the CRM is the operational record.

### The bar a teacher must meet
A teacher is approved only if all four are true:
1. **Real person, reachable.** A working email (and phone if she gave one) that we have actually corresponded with. We have had a real reply from her, not just a form fill.
2. **Currently teaches American Mahjong.** She confirms she actively teaches, and we have at least one external signal: an OMM relationship, an NMJL credential, an existing class listing at a senior center, library, JCC, or rec center, a class flyer, a website, or a personal vouch from someone we trust.
3. **Facts are sound.** In conversation she demonstrates she knows the game (American Mahjong: 152 tiles, Jokers are wild, the card comes out every spring). We are not testing her, just confirming she is a genuine teacher and not someone who will mislead players.
4. **Agrees to our safety rules.** New groups meet in public places. We never publish home addresses or personal phone numbers. First names only in public. She is fine with all of it.

### The step-by-step admin workflow

1. **Capture.** When a teacher replies positively, create or open her record in the Instructors table (tblvkXhCbWli8HKpF). Fill Name, Email, Phone, Specialties, Preferred Communication, and the source in Notes (OMM, NMJL, local, Airtable). Leave **Active Status unchecked** until vetting is complete. This is the holding state.

2. **Verify realness and teaching.** Confirm the two-way reply, and log the external signal in Notes (for example: "Teaches Tuesday class at {{venue}}, Henderson" or "NMJL instructor, confirmed at OMM 2025"). No signal, no listing yet, ask for one.

3. **Safety confirmation.** Confirm in writing (a line in her email reply is enough) that she agrees to public-venue, first-names-only, no-home-address listings. Note it.

4. **Approve.** When all four bars are met, check **Active Status** in the Instructors table. This is the single switch that means "vetted and listable." Set Instructor Since to today.

5. **Publish behind login.** In the admin teacher workflow, create her public listing using first name, neighborhoods served, and specialties only. Never her address or personal phone. Double-check no private contact info is exposed before it goes live.

6. **Activate.** Send the student handoff link and the Message 3 offer confirmation. Create a Follow-Up record (Follow-Ups table) to check in at 14 days: did her students find games, is she sharing the link.

7. **Track to incentives.** As her students appear in Attendance and her groups appear in Sessions, the referral ladder in Part 4 unlocks naturally from real CRM activity. When she crosses the Active or Ambassador gate, send the matching note and update Tags on her People record.

### Realness and safety checklist (run before checking Active Status)

- [ ] We have a real two-way reply from this person (not just a form fill).
- [ ] At least one external signal confirms she currently teaches American Mahjong.
- [ ] Her American Mahjong facts are sound in conversation.
- [ ] She has agreed in writing to public venues, first names only, no home address, no personal phone in public.
- [ ] Her listing uses first name and neighborhoods only, with no private contact info exposed.
- [ ] Source and verification note logged in the Instructors record.

If any box is unchecked, she stays in the holding state (Active Status unchecked) until it clears. Speed matters, but a bad listing costs us the trust we are trying to build. The two are not in conflict: vetting a real teacher takes one honest email exchange and five minutes in the CRM.
