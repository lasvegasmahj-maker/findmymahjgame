# 90-Day Wartime Execution Plan

Ruthless prioritization for the next 90 days, plus the team design the brief asked for, with an honest challenge to it. The wartime truth: we are not a five-team company, we are a founder with a working platform and a goldmine of warm Las Vegas relationships. The fastest path to becoming the place every American mahjong player checks is not to build a hub with five teams. It is to win a few cities with mostly manual effort, then repeat.

## Challenge to the five-team frame

The brief says "assume we can deploy multiple teams simultaneously." We cannot, and pretending we can is how focus dies. Treat the five teams as five workstreams, and notice that only two are active now. The rest are mostly paused or manual.

| Team / workstream | Active now? | Reality for the next 90 days |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | Yes, the founder | The whole game right now: mine Las Vegas assets, recruit and activate ambassadors, form recurring tables |
| Community | Yes, the founder plus ambassadors | The weekly local newsletter and keeping recurring games alive |
| Partnership | Light, manual | Line up free public venues in launch cities; no paid program yet |
| Product | Mostly paused | Platform is good enough; only tiny fixes that directly help table formation |
| Data | Use what exists | Read the dashboards already shipped (attribution, heat map, pipeline); no new build |

So the "five teams" are really one founder doing Growth and Community, with light manual Partnership, and Product and Data effectively paused. Designing elaborate mandates for teams that do not exist would be theater.

## The team designs (as requested, with realistic mandates)

Growth (the founder, now):
- Mission: form recurring tables in Las Vegas from existing relationships.
- Deliverables: 5 recurring Las Vegas tables in 30 days; at least 5 activated ambassadors; a rising organic ratio.
- Timeline: weeks 1-4 Las Vegas; weeks 5-12 extend the same motion to Phoenix and Boca.
- Dependencies: the existing CRM and email list; the live attribution and pipeline tools.

Community (the founder plus ambassadors, now):
- Mission: make players return weekly and feel part of a scene.
- Deliverables: launch Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly this week (manual); keep every recurring game alive; capture community stories.
- Timeline: weekly from now.
- Dependencies: the existing email list; real local activity to write about.

Partnership (light, manual):
- Mission: secure safe public places to play.
- Deliverables: one or two free public venues (library, community center, senior center) per launch city.
- Timeline: as each city launches.
- Dependencies: an ambassador in the city.

Product (mostly paused):
- Mission: stay out of the way; remove only friction that blocks table formation.
- Deliverables: small fixes if and only if they directly increase tables formed or filled. No new features.
- Timeline: ad hoc.
- Dependencies: evidence from real use that something blocks a table.

Data (use what exists):
- Mission: tell the truth about what is working.
- Deliverables: weekly review of attribution, heat map, pipeline, and the founder-assisted vs organic ratio.
- Timeline: every Friday.
- Dependencies: none new; the tools are live.

## Part 8: the ruthless 90-day calls

Build immediately (this week):
- Nothing new in software. The platform is enough.
- Launch Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly, manually, from the existing list. (This is content, not a build.)
- Stand up the first recurring tables from existing hosts and students.

Operate manually before building software:
- The newsletter (hand-written weekly).
- The event calendar (a section inside the newsletter, hand-curated).
- Venue partnerships (an ambassador asks a library; a handshake, not a program).
- Teacher and venue lists (a simple sheet as you meet them; admin tools already exist to enter them).
- Ambassador recruiting and onboarding (personal calls; the pipeline tool tracks them).

Wait (Phase 2, after a city is dense):
- Public teacher directory, ambassador profiles, and city pages (already built behind admin; publish when real).
- A real event calendar product.
- City newsletters beyond Las Vegas.

Kill or shelve (for 90 days):
- The national event calendar as a near-term build.
- A national newsletter (do city-first).
- Tournaments, leagues, cruises, retreats as builds (listings or partnerships only, later).
- The paid mahjong-friendly business program.
- Influencer tiers B and C, SMS, PWA polish, icon redesigns, and the advertiser and monetization funnel.
- Producing more strategy documents. We now have a full library. Further planning is procrastination; execute.

Delegate (off the founder's plate):
- Hosting of recurring tables: hand each table to a non-founder host as fast as possible (this is the organic-ratio goal).
- Parts of the newsletter and local content: to ambassadors once they are active.
- City launches beyond Las Vegas: to the local ambassador, with the founder coaching, not doing.

## The 90-day arc

- Days 1-30: Las Vegas. Mine existing assets, launch the weekly newsletter, stand up 5 recurring tables, activate 5 ambassadors. Prove the loop and the return-visit habit in one city.
- Days 31-60: deepen Las Vegas (more recurring tables, rising organic ratio, first non-founder hosts) and start Phoenix and Boca with the same manual playbook and a local newsletter each.
- Days 61-90: Las Vegas largely self-sustaining and handed to hosts and an ambassador captain; Phoenix and Boca each with at least one recurring game and a local newsletter. Decide, on evidence, whether to expand to the next cities.

## The only scoreboard for 90 days

Recurring tables, tables filled, games played, activated ambassadors, and the organic ratio, per city. If a proposed activity does not move one of those this quarter, it waits. The national hub is the destination; winning Las Vegas, then a few more cities, mostly by hand, is the road. Stay on the road.
