# 30 / 60 / 90 Day Execution Plan

Concrete, ranked against the five priorities (Las Vegas table formation, ambassador recruitment, teacher outreach, newsletter launch, event and open-play inventory). Mostly non-product, mostly manual. The homepage is frozen; the product is enough; the work is relationships and activity. The only scoreboard: Tables Created, Tables Filled, Games Played, Recurring Activity.

## First 30 days: prove Las Vegas

Goal: 5 recurring Las Vegas tables, the weekly newsletter live, the first ambassadors activated.

- P1 Tables: mine the existing email list and CRM. Put the first existing-host group on the tool (fastest recurring table). Invite students and open-play attendees to specific tables. Stand up 5 tables; fill and play them; set them recurring.
- P2 Ambassadors: call every existing host and organizer; sign 2 to 4; start their first table on the call. Track all in the relationship pipeline.
- P3 Teachers: contact local and Oh My Mahjong teachers as ambassador recruits; enter them in the admin teacher tool.
- P4 Newsletter: launch Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly by hand from the existing list. One issue a week: tables needing a 4th, new tables, open plays, one tip, one story, one call to action.
- P5 Activity: collect every open play and table into the newsletter and a simple list. This is the start of the activity feed.
- Build: nothing new. Use attribution, heat map, and pipeline weekly.

End-of-30 check: 5 recurring tables, at least 5 activated ambassadors, 4 newsletter issues sent, a rising organic ratio.

## Days 31 to 60: deepen Las Vegas, open the launch cities

Goal: Las Vegas approaching self-sustaining; Phoenix and Boca started.

- P1: more recurring tables in Las Vegas; hand each filled table to a non-founder host (raise the organic ratio).
- P2: recruit the Las Vegas ambassador captain; sign Hot ambassadors in Phoenix and Boca; start their first tables.
- P3: continue teacher outreach; approach the 20-real-teacher bar so the directory can eventually publish.
- P4: keep Las Vegas Weekly going; start a manual weekly note for Phoenix and Boca once they have any activity.
- P5: grow the activity inventory (open plays, lessons) in Las Vegas; begin in the launch cities.
- Build (only if proven necessary): a minimal activity-first "find mahjong near you" view that surfaces real local tables and open plays (location in, nearby activity out, one action). Build this only if there is enough real activity that a list beats the current flow, and keep it senior-simple with no filter wall.

End-of-60 check: Las Vegas mostly host-run; Phoenix and Boca each forming tables; activity inventory growing; organic ratio clearly up.

## Days 61 to 90: self-sustaining and decide

Goal: Las Vegas runs without the founder; the launch cities have recurring games; an evidence-based expansion decision.

- P1: Las Vegas recurring games continue without founder pushing; the organic ratio is the headline metric.
- P2: ambassadors recruit the next hosts themselves; the founder coaches, not does.
- P3: if the teacher count is real and at the bar, publish the teacher directory (the first Phase 2 surface to go live).
- P4: city newsletters established in Las Vegas and started in the launch cities.
- P5: enough open-play and table activity that an activity-first discovery view is genuinely useful in Las Vegas (a Phase 2 candidate to ship).
- Decide: on evidence, whether to open the next cities, using the heat map to choose where demand is already real.

End-of-90 check: at least one fully won city (recurring games, host-run, a living local activity feed and newsletter), launch cities in progress, and a clear, data-backed plan for the next quarter.

## What to build, wait, kill, delegate, operate manually

- Build immediately: nothing new in software. Launch the newsletter (content, not a build).
- Operate manually first: newsletter, event and open-play calendar (inside the newsletter), venue partnerships, teacher and activity lists, ambassador recruiting and onboarding.
- Wait (Phase 2): activity-first discovery view, public teacher directory, ambassador profiles, city pages, city newsletters beyond Las Vegas.
- Kill or shelve (90 days): venue directory as a product, filter-heavy discovery, national calendar software, national newsletter, tournaments and leagues and cruises and retreats as builds, the paid business program, SMS, install prompts, advertiser funnel, and further strategy documents.
- Delegate: hosting of recurring tables to non-founder hosts; local content to active ambassadors; launch-city execution to local ambassadors with founder coaching.

## The mandate

For 90 days, the work is to seat real people at recurring Las Vegas mahjong tables, hand those tables to others to run, give players a weekly reason to come back (the newsletter and a living local activity feed), and repeat the motion in two more cities. Everything is ranked against the five priorities and the four metrics. If a task does not move one of them this quarter, it waits.
