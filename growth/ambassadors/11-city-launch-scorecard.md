# City Launch Scorecard

City Launch Mode. We are proving the playbook in 3 markets before national expansion. This scorecard tracks every launch city on one page so we always know which cities have momentum and which need help this week.

North Star: Tables Formed, then Filled, then Played, then Recurring. A city is winning when tables are forming and filling on a standing schedule, not when an ambassador is merely signed.

## The metrics (per city)

1. Ambassador Applications. People from this city who applied.
2. Ambassadors Approved. Approved or in active conversation.
3. Activated Ambassadors. Approved ambassadors who have started at least one real table. This is the metric that predicts everything else.
4. Tables Created. Tables started in the city.
5. Tables Filled. Tables that reached 4 of 4 players.
6. Games Played. Tables confirmed played.
7. Recurring Tables. Tables that play on a standing day and time.

## Red / Yellow / Green status system

Set each city's status by its most honest signal against where it should be for its week.

| Status | Meaning | Set it when | What it triggers |
|--------|---------|-------------|------------------|
| Green | Momentum, on or ahead of plan | At least 1 activated ambassador, tables are filling (1+ filled or clearly on pace), and a recurring game is in motion or close | Document and amplify. Capture what worked, ask for a short testimonial, consider adding a second ambassador. |
| Yellow | Slowing, needs attention this week | Ambassador approved but not yet activated and still inside the first 2 weeks, OR tables created but none filling, OR one step behind the weekly milestone | Founder coaching this week. Push the launch-kit invite scripts, set a specific date for the next table, offer to do it together. |
| Red | Stalled or at risk, intervene now | No activated ambassador by end of Week 2, OR no tables created, OR the ambassador has gone quiet, OR a green city made no North Star progress for 2 weeks | Founder intervenes directly: start a table live with them on a call, or decide to replace the ambassador. |

Rule of thumb: the dominant signal is activation plus table momentum versus the week. A signed ambassador with no table in Week 3 is Red, no matter how enthusiastic.

## How to run it

- Update Friday, right after the Founder Weekly Dashboard.
- Pull Applications and Approved from the admin Ambassadors tab. Pull Activated and the table metrics from /admin/metrics and the ambassador tracking sheet. Product data wins over the sheet.
- For every Yellow, write the one coaching action for the week. For every Red, write the intervention or the replacement decision. Do not leave a Red without a decision.
- Celebrate every Green with the ambassador by name (first name only).

## What "proven" means for these 3 cities

The playbook is proven in a city when, within about 30 days, the city reaches Green and holds it: at least one activated ambassador, multiple tables formed and filled, and at least one recurring game that keeps playing without the founder pushing it. When all 3 markets hit that bar, we expand nationally using the same motion.
