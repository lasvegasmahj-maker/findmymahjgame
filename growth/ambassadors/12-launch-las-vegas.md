# City Launch Plan: Las Vegas

Primary proof market. This is the home market, with an existing audience through the sister site lasvegasmahj.com and the founder on the ground. Las Vegas is also a year-round residential city, so unlike the snowbird markets, a summer launch works here. Prove the playbook here first, then carry the exact motion to the others.

## Targets (30 days)

- Ambassador target: 3 (one lead ambassador plus 2 co-hosts in different parts of the valley, for example Summerlin and Henderson/Green Valley and Anthem).
- Table target: 10 tables created, 6 filled, 3 recurring.
- Players reached: 25 or more distinct players.
- Status goal: reach Green by Day 21 and hold it.

## Why these targets

This is the easiest market to over-deliver in because of the home advantage and the existing lasvegasmahj.com audience. Set the bar high here so the playbook we carry to other cities is proven against a strong result, not a lucky one.

## 30-day launch checklist

### Week 0: Setup (about 30 minutes)
- [ ] Confirm the lead ambassador (warmest, highest-reach local contact).
- [ ] Assign referral code, for example FMM-LV-{FIRSTNAME}.
- [ ] Identify 2 candidate co-hosts in different neighborhoods.
- [ ] Note 3 candidate public venues (a library, a community center, a cafe with table space) across Summerlin, Henderson, and the northwest.

### Week 1: First tables and the lead ambassador
- [ ] Lead ambassador starts their first table on /start.
- [ ] Founder starts 1 table personally using the lasvegasmahj.com audience.
- [ ] Invite the existing lasvegasmahj.com list to claim seats.
- [ ] Sign at least 1 co-host and onboard them.
- [ ] Milestone: 2 tables created, 1 filling.

### Week 2: Fill and add a neighborhood
- [ ] Lead ambassador fills their first table (4 of 4).
- [ ] Co-host starts a table in a second neighborhood.
- [ ] Lock 1 public venue for a first group meet.
- [ ] Sign the third ambassador.
- [ ] Milestone: 4 tables created, 2 filled, first game played.

### Week 3: Open play and first recurring game
- [ ] Run 1 beginner-friendly open session at a public venue.
- [ ] Turn the first filled table into a recurring game (standing day and time).
- [ ] Co-hosts each have a table filling.
- [ ] Milestone: 7 tables created, 4 filled, 1 recurring.

### Week 4: Density and documentation
- [ ] Three neighborhoods each have at least one filled table.
- [ ] At least 3 recurring games scheduled.
- [ ] Capture what worked: best invite script, best venue type, best day and time.
- [ ] Write the 1-page "how Las Vegas launched" summary to reuse in the next cities.
- [ ] Milestone: 10 tables created, 6 filled, 3 recurring. Status Green.

## Risks

1. Founder bias. Because this is home, it is easy to carry the city personally and not actually test whether an ambassador can run it. That hides whether the playbook works.
2. Summer heat. Outdoor or non-climate-controlled venues will not work in a Las Vegas summer.
3. Transient players. Some Las Vegas players are seasonal or travel; a table can lose a player.
4. Leaning on tourists. Tourist players do not create recurring games. We need locals.

## Recovery actions

1. For founder bias: after Week 1, deliberately step back and let the lead ambassador run their table. Measure their tables separately from the founder's. The playbook is only proven if an ambassador, not the founder, drives the Green.
2. For heat: book only indoor, air-conditioned venues (libraries, community centers, senior centers, cafes). Make this the default in the venue list.
3. For transient players: keep each table list one or two players deep on a waitlist via the site, so a drop is filled fast. Encourage recurring games so the group becomes sticky.
4. For tourist reliance: prioritize the lasvegasmahj.com local audience and neighborhood groups, not Strip or visitor channels. Count only local players toward the targets.
