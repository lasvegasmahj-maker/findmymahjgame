# Top 25 Target Cities

Goal: recruit 25 Founding Ambassadors in 25 cities. This is the prioritized list of where to plant them first.

## The single most important rule

A warm relationship beats a good demographic every time. If Shauna met a strong teacher, studio owner, or organizer from a city at the Oh My Mahjong conference, that city jumps to the top of the list no matter where it ranks below. Use this list to fill the gaps and to decide between two equally warm contacts, not to override a real relationship.

## How these are ranked

Each city is scored on five signals. We do not have exact counts for most of these, so the scores are strategic estimates (High, Medium, Low) based on well known demographics and mahjong community patterns. Validate the top candidates against two real sources before committing: (1) your Oh My Mahjong contact list, and (2) a quick search for local mahjong classes, clubs, and NMJL teachers in that metro.

1. Mahjong activity. Visible classes, clubs, open plays, and groups already running.
2. Existing teacher presence. NMJL instructors who can seed tables and bring students.
3. Retirement population. Large, active 55+ communities who play during the day and play often.
4. Community concentration. Affluent suburbs, JCCs, country clubs, active-adult communities, and Asian-American and Jewish communities where the game has deep roots.
5. Likelihood of rapid adoption. Existing social infrastructure (senior centers, libraries, community centers) and a culture of organized recreation.

Do not treat any number in this file as a verified statistic. These are priorities, not measurements.

## How to use this list

1. Lay your Oh My Mahjong contacts over this list. Mark every city where you have a warm name.
2. Fill the 25 slots: warm-contact cities first, then the highest-ranked cities without a contact yet.
3. For the no-contact cities, use the Recruiting Playbook to source a candidate (search local NMJL teachers, JCC game coordinators, active-adult community activity directors, library program staff).
4. Confirm each city before you commit by checking for at least one of: an existing teacher, an existing group, or a willing venue partner.
5. Track all 25 in one simple sheet (see the Referral Tracking Plan). One row per city, one ambassador per row.

## A note on honesty

When we publish anything public about a city (a page, a post, a claim), it must be true and sourced. This file is an internal targeting tool, so it uses estimates. Do not copy these signal ratings into public marketing as if they were measured facts.
