# National Mahjong Hub Roadmap

Is the long-term destination "the place to find mahjong players" or "the place where mahjong players discover what is happening." The honest answer: it is both, in sequence. Finding players and forming tables is the foundation and the first habit; discovering local activity is the layer that makes people return. You cannot have the discovery layer without first creating the activity, and you create the activity by forming tables. So: players and tables first, activity discovery second, the broad national hub last.

## Reframing the destination

The most accurate long-term framing is local and activity-first: "the place where I find what mahjong is happening near me, and the people I play it with." Not a national directory of everything, and not only a player finder. It is a living local activity feed (tables, open plays, lessons, events) plus the relationships that produce it, repeated city by city until it is national.

This resolves the either-or: "find players" is how it starts and what seeds the supply; "discover what is happening" is what it becomes once there is enough local activity to discover. One leads to the other. Build them in that order.

## Phase 1: prove player discovery and recurring tables (now)

The foundation. Without it, there is nothing to discover.
- Player discovery (I want to play), table formation (start a table), and need a 4th. Already live.
- Local ambassadors as the engine. Live and being recruited.
- Recurring tables in Las Vegas from existing relationships. The proof.
- The weekly local newsletter (manual) as the first return-visit habit.
Success: the first 5 recurring Las Vegas tables and a rising organic ratio. Nothing else gets built.

## Phase 2: become local mahjong discovery (per dense city)

Once a city has real activity, add the discovery layer on top of it.
- Activity-first discovery: find mahjong near you, showing real local open plays, tables, and lessons (not a venue directory; see the Venue vs Activity analysis).
- Open plays and the local event inventory (priority 5) become the changing weekly content that drives return.
- Teachers as a published directory (once about 20 are real) and as a player supply.
- The local newsletter matures; venues are secured as free hosting (operations, not a paid program yet).
Success: in one city, a player can find a game, an open play, and a teacher, all real, and comes back weekly to see what changed.

## Phase 3: become the national hub (later)

Replicate local hubs and layer on the episodic and revenue elements.
- Tournaments, leagues, retreats, and cruises as listings and partnerships (not builds), surfaced through the activity feed.
- Mahjong-friendly business program as the revenue layer (featured venues, sponsors), sold into engaged local audiences.
- Local newsletters in many cities, with a light national digest on top.
- National calendar as an aggregation of city activity, only once city-level activity is dense.
Success: the default national destination, built from many strong local hubs, not a thin national directory.

## The ten elements, mapped

| Element | Phase | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Player discovery / find players | 1 | The foundation and first habit |
| Local ambassadors | 1 | The engine that produces activity |
| Recurring tables | 1 | The proof and the deepest moat |
| Local newsletter | 1 (manual), 2 (per city) | The return-visit habit, cheap and now |
| Open plays | 2 | The changing weekly activity that drives return |
| Teachers | 2 | Relationships now, directory at scale |
| Mahjong-friendly businesses | 2-3 | Free hosting now, paid program later |
| Tournaments and leagues | 3 | Listings and partnerships, episodic |
| Retreats and cruises | 3 | Partnerships and affiliate, infrequent |
| National layer / calendar | 3 | Aggregation of won cities, not a starting build |

## The discipline this roadmap demands

The vision is large; the next move is small. The fastest path to the national hub is not to build it; it is to make Las Vegas so locally alive (recurring games, fresh weekly activity, a local note) that checking the platform becomes a habit, then repeat in the next cities. Resist building Phase 2 and 3 features before Phase 1 is proven. The hub is the sum of won cities, and right now we have zero won cities. Win one.
