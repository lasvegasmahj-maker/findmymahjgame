## 1. The Exact Process: How to Find and Aggregate Events

Work the sources in this order. Earlier sources are warmer and faster. Always capture into the tracker (Section 5) the moment you find something, even if it is incomplete. A half-filled row is worth more than a perfect memory.

### Source A: The Oh My Mahjong Network (warmest, do first)
These are people who already know Shauna. They convert fastest and they each know dozens more events.

- Open your contacts and notes from the Oh My Mahjong conference. List every teacher, organizer, and studio owner you spoke with.
- For each one, ask directly: "What do you run, and what do you know about?" One teacher often surfaces 3-10 events (their own classes, a club they play in, a tournament they attend, a retreat they teach at).
- Use the warm organizer script (Section 3) by email or text.
- Log each person in the tracker as a source even before they reply, so nobody falls through.

### Source B: The Airtable CRM (already yours)
You are sitting on a goldmine. Mine it before going outside.

- **Instructors table:** Every instructor is an event source. Teachers run classes, host open plays, and know the local scene. Go row by row. For each instructor, create at least one tracker row for "their teaching schedule" and reach out with the open-play / teacher script.
- **Organizations table:** Every organization (library, senior center, club, studio, JCC, retirement community, recreation center) is a venue and likely an event host. For each one, check whether they run a recurring mahjong session. Create a tracker row per known or suspected event.
- **Sessions table:** If you have logged past or recurring Las Vegas sessions, each recurring one is already an event. List them all now. These are your easiest wins.
- **People table:** Filter for anyone tagged as a host, ambassador, or frequent player. Ask each: "Where do you play, and who runs it?"

### Source C: Public Facebook Groups and Club Pages
Public, searchable, and where seniors actually post game days.

- Search Facebook for: "mahjong Las Vegas," "mahjong Henderson," "mahjong Summerlin," "American Mahjong [city]," "Sun City mahjong," "55+ mahjong," and the same for retreats and tournaments nationally.
- Join or follow public groups. Read pinned posts and recent posts. Recurring game days are usually posted by an admin.
- Capture: event name, day, time, venue (public name only), city, and the admin's first name. Do not capture or store anyone's home address or personal phone number.
- Message the group admin with the organizer script asking permission to list the public event.

### Source D: Libraries and Senior Centers (the public-venue backbone)
These are safe, public, recurring, and easy to verify. They are the spine of local inventory.

- Pull the public events calendars for each library system and senior center in the Las Vegas geography: Summerlin, Henderson and Green Valley, Anthem and Sun City, Centennial Hills, Spring Valley, and central Las Vegas.
- Look for "mahjong," "mah jongg," "game day," "card and tile games," and "open play."
- These calendars are public, so you can list the event immediately and then send a courtesy note to the activities coordinator (organizer script) letting them know you have listed it and offering to help fill seats.
- Repeat nationally for any library or senior center surfaced by your network.

### Source E: National Tournament, Retreat, and Cruise Operators
This is what makes us national and the source of truth, not just a Las Vegas board.

- List every recurring tournament, mahjong retreat, mahjong cruise, and multi-day event you or your network know of.
- Find their public registration pages and dates.
- Reach out with the tournament/retreat operator script. These operators want listings because it fills seats. Offer to be their free national listing.
- A single national operator can add 5-15 dated events to inventory in one reply.

### Capture discipline (applies to every source)
- The moment you find anything, create a tracker row. Status can be "Lead."
- Public sources (library/senior center calendars, public Facebook posts, public registration pages) can be listed as "Listed - public source" and verified, because they are already public. Always confirm the venue is a public place.
- Private or member-only groups: do not list until the host says yes (use scripts).
- First names only in public. Public venue names only. No home addresses. No personal phone numbers.

## 4. The Submission Process (Submit an Event)

Keep it manual and tiny. Two ways in, one human vetting pass, safe display.

### Two intake paths
1. **Email:** Anyone can submit an event by emailing **hello@findmymahjgame.com** with the subject "Submit an event." Promote this everywhere (newsletter, scripts, the site footer).
2. **Basic form:** A simple form at **findmymahjgame.com/start** (or a no-frills embedded form) collecting exactly the tracker fields below. No login, no account. If a quick build is not ready, the email path alone is enough for the next 30 days.

**Submission fields (keep it short):**
- Event name
- Type (open play / class / tournament / retreat / cruise / one-off)
- Day and time (or dates)
- City and neighborhood
- Public venue name
- Host first name
- Public link or "have players reply to us"
- Cost (free / drop-in fee / registration)
- Anything players should know (skill level, bring your own card, etc.)

### Vetting for realness and safety (one human pass, under 5 minutes each)
Before anything goes public, Shauna or an ambassador checks:
1. **Real?** Does the venue exist and is it a public place (library, community center, senior center, cafe, studio, recreation center)? A quick search confirms it. Public calendar or registration page = confirmed.
2. **Safe venue?** If the submitted location is a home address or anything private, do not publish the address. Reply and ask for a public meeting place, or list it as "location shared on request" and route interested players through us.
3. **No private data?** Strip any home address and personal phone number. First names only. Public link or our contact only.
4. **Accurate facts?** If the listing describes the game, make sure American Mahjong details are right (152 tiles; Bams, Craks, Dots; Jokers are wild; the NMJL card releases every spring). Fix anything wrong.
5. **Permission for member-only events?** If it came from a private group, confirm the host wants it listed.

### Status flow (use the tracker's Status column)
`Lead` (found, not confirmed) → `Verified` (real and safe, ready) → `Listed` (live on site) → `Needs update` (date passed or details changed) → `Archived` (no longer running).

Public-source events (library calendars, public registration pages) can move from Lead straight to Verified the same day, because they are already public. Anything touching a private host waits for a yes.

## 6. Weekly Goals (Week 1 to 4)

Each week ladders up to the 150-event, 25-city target. Front-load the warm and public sources, then go national.

### Week 1: Mine what you already have (warm + public)
**Theme:** Empty your own pockets first. Stand up the tracker.
- Build the Events Tracker (Section 5) in Airtable. Create the six views.
- Mine the Airtable CRM: log every Instructor, Organization, and recurring Session as tracker rows.
- Pull and list every public library and senior center mahjong session across the Las Vegas geography.
- Send the organizer script to your top 15 warm Oh My Mahjong contacts.
- **Targets:** 45 events listed or verified, 20 venues mapped, 10 teachers logged, 8 cities, 25 open plays, 2 tournaments.

### Week 2: Work the network and Las Vegas Facebook
**Theme:** Turn warm replies into listings; sweep public local groups.
- Follow up with Week 1 contacts. Log every new event they surface.
- Search and join public Facebook mahjong groups for the Las Vegas neighborhoods. List public recurring games; message admins for the rest.
- Send the open-play host script to every Las Vegas host you have found.

### Week 3: Go national (operators)
**Theme:** One email to an operator = many dated events.
- Send the tournament/retreat operator script to every national operator on your list. Ask each for their full calendar.
- Add every dated tournament, retreat, and cruise to the National calendar view.

- Verify and flip Week 1-2 leads to Listed.
- **Targets (cumulative):** 120 events, 36 venues, 25 teachers, 20 cities, 50 open plays, 15 tournaments, 9 retreats, 5 cruises.

### Week 4: Fill gaps, verify, and turn on submissions
**Theme:** Close to 150, make it trustworthy, open the front door.

- **Targets (cumulative, the 30-day finish line):** 150 events, 40 venues, 30 teachers, 25 cities, 60 open plays, 20 tournaments, 12 retreats, 6 cruises.

## Daily Rhythm (so the targets actually happen)

A simple founder's loop, about 60-90 minutes a day:
1. **Find (20 min):** Work one source from Section 1. Create tracker rows immediately.
2. **Reach out (20 min):** Send 5-10 scripts. Always log the contact as a source.
3. **Verify and list (20 min):** Move yesterday's leads through Verified to Listed. Safety-check every one.
4. **Follow up (10 min):** Nudge anyone who has not replied in 3 days with the one-liner.

Speed beats perfection. A listed event with a public day, time, and venue is a win. Keep the rows flowing, keep it safe, and in 30 days we are the largest and most trusted collection of American Mahjong events online.
