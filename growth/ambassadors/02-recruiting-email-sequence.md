# Ambassador Recruiting Email Sequence

A complete, ready-to-send outbound sequence to turn Oh My Mahjong conference contacts and warm leads into Founding Ambassador applicants for FindMyMahj (findmymahjgame.com).

**The goal of every email is the same:** one click to apply at **findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors**. FindMyMahj is a free national service that helps people find mahjong players and form real games. Ambassadors help people in their town start tables and fill seats. There is nothing to sell and no cost to anyone.

**The North Star behind all of this:** Tables Formed, then Tables Filled, then Games Played, then Recurring Tables. One ambassador can start many tables, which brings in many players, which becomes regular games.

---

## How to use this sequence

- **Five emails over 20 days.** Send Day 0, Day 3, Day 7, Day 12, and Day 20. If someone replies or applies at any point, stop the sequence and switch to the reply-handling snippet at the bottom.
- **Tokens.** Replace `{{first_name}}`, `{{city}}`, and `{{your_name}}` before sending. `{{your_name}}` is the FindMyMahj team member sending the email.
- **Personas.** This sequence works for all four ambassador types: Teachers (mahjong instructors), Studio owners, Organizers (club or community organizers), and Event hosts. Where a line can be sharpened for a specific type, a short note in brackets shows how. Pick the version that fits the person and delete the bracketed note.
- **From and reply-to.** Send from a real person at hello@findmymahjgame.com so replies come straight back to a human.

**Personalization note (do this for every email):** add one true, specific detail near the top, such as the city they teach in, a table they mentioned at the conference, or a comment they made. One real line of personalization does more than a whole page of polish.

## Email 2 (Day 3): How it actually works

**Subject:** How a table fills up on FindMyMahj
**Alternate subject:** What being an ambassador actually looks like

Hi {{first_name}},

I wanted to show you how simple this is, since "ambassador" can sound like a big commitment. It is not.

Here is the whole thing:

1. You share your link with a few players in {{city}}.
2. Someone starts a table at findmymahjgame.com/start. They pick a day, a time, and their general area or ZIP. No home address, ever. They give a first name and a phone or email, and they take the first seat.
3. The table page shows a simple seat counter, like "3 of 4 players, 1 seat to go," with a "Need a 4th" badge when one seat is left.
4. When the fourth seat fills, the site automatically emails all four players to set up the first game in a public place. Contact info is never shared between players, and replies go to the host as the coordinator.

That is it. You are not managing schedules or chasing people. The site does the coordinating. You just help good games get started, and every table from your link is credited to you.

When you are ready, the next step is the same short form:

**Apply here: findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors**

Warmly,
{{your_name}}
FindMyMahj

*P.S. New groups always meet in a public place for the first game. It keeps everyone comfortable, which matters a lot for first-time players.*

> [Organizer version: "If you already run a club, your members can fill a new table in a day."]

**"What if I am not very technical?"** That is fine. Everything runs from a phone in a few taps, and we are here to help you.

If those answers land the way I hope, here is the one step:

## Reply-handling snippet (when someone says yes)

Use this the moment a contact replies with interest, before or instead of the next scheduled email. Keep it short and warm, and point them to the one step.

> Subject: Wonderful, {{first_name}}, here is the one step
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> This made my day. Welcome aboard.
>
> The only thing to do now is fill out the short application so we can set up your ambassador link: **findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors**. It takes a couple of minutes and asks for your name, email, city, and a little about the players you can reach.
>
> Right after you submit, you will get a confirmation email from us, and our team will follow up to get your link and first tables going. If anything is unclear, just reply here and I will walk you through it.
>
> So glad to have you, {{first_name}}.
>
> Warmly,
> {{your_name}}
> FindMyMahj

**If they reply with a question instead of a clear yes:** answer the one question in two or three sentences, then close with the same single call to action, for example: "Once that is settled, the next step is the short form at findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors, and I will take it from there."

**If they reply "not right now":** thank them, tell them the door stays open, and stop the sequence. For example: "Totally understood, {{first_name}}. The link will be here whenever the timing is right, and it was great connecting at Oh My Mahjong."
