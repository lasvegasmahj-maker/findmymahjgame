# 30-Day Focus Plan

Product is no longer the bottleneck. Relationships, players, hosts, and ambassadors are. For the next 30 days we do almost nothing except turn existing Las Vegas relationships into recurring tables. This plan is deliberately narrow. Everything is ranked by impact on tables formed, filled, played, and recurring.

## The honest challenge: what to stop

We have been building and planning faster than we have been forming tables. The platform, the admin tools, the dashboards, the city plans, and the strategy library are all good enough to prove the model. More of them will not create a single table. Be ruthless and pause the following for 30 days.

Pause, no impact on tables right now:
1. Teacher Directory, Ambassador Profiles, and City Pages (public build). They depend on real data we do not have yet. Keep the admin workflows ready, but do not build more here.
2. Need a Sub. There are not enough recurring tables to sub for. It is a solution to a problem we do not have yet.
3. Influencer strategy Tiers B and C (creators, podcasters, lifestyle). These create traffic, not tables, and the goal is not traffic.
4. SMS / Twilio, PWA polish, homepage icon redesigns, and any new product features. Nice-to-haves that do not seat players.
5. The advertiser and monetization funnel. Revenue is not the goal now.
6. Producing more strategy documents and plans. We have enough. Writing another plan is procrastination dressed as progress. Execute the ones we have.
7. Opening new cities beyond the three. Go deep in Las Vegas first.

Distraction test for anything not on the keep list: "Will this seat four real players at a recurring table in the next 30 days?" If no, it waits.

## What to keep (ranked by table impact)

1. Mine existing Las Vegas assets (doc 22). Highest ROI. Warm, local, ready players already in our CRM and email list.
2. Stand up the first 5 recurring tables (doc 23). The concrete 30-day outcome.
3. Activate warm relationships into ambassadors (doc 25), Las Vegas first. Hosts who run tables without the founder.
4. Convert founder-assisted tables to organic (doc 24). The proof the model scales.
5. Use the tools we already shipped weekly: attribution (/admin/metrics), heat map (/admin/heatmap), and the relationship pipeline (/admin/pipeline). Decisions over guesses.

Everything else is paused.

## The 30-day founder schedule

Week 1, light the fastest fuses:
- Put your existing-host group on the tool (Table 4). It is the quickest recurring table.
- Call every existing host and organizer in the CRM. Sign 2 to 3 as ambassadors.
- Invite students and open-play attendees to Tables 1 and 2.
- Send the newsletter broadcast to surface demand by area.
- Outcome: 3 to 4 tables forming, 2 to 3 ambassadors signed.

Week 2, fill and add:
- Fill Tables 1, 2, and 4 to 4 of 4; play the first games; set them recurring immediately.
- Start Table 3 with an active-adult community partner.
- Call Hot launch-city contacts (Phoenix, Boca) to plant the next markets.
- Outcome: 3 tables filled and played, first recurring games set.

Week 3, make it stick and recruit hosts:
- Confirm Tables 1 to 4 are recurring (played twice).
- Form Table 5 from the newsletter demand cluster.
- For each filled table, name a non-founder host and start handing it over.
- Outcome: 4 recurring tables, first organic host running a table.

Week 4, prove and measure:
- All 5 tables filled and recurring (played twice).
- Aim for at least 3 of 5 hosted by someone other than the founder.
- Friday review: update the Founder-Assisted vs Organic dashboard, the City Scorecard, and the pipeline.
- Outcome: 5 recurring Las Vegas tables, a rising organic ratio, and a repeatable motion to carry to Phoenix and Boca.

## The only metrics that matter for 30 days

- Recurring tables (target: 5 in Las Vegas).
- Tables filled and games played (the leading indicators).
- Activated ambassadors (target: at least 5).
- Organic ratio (should start rising by week 3).

Ignore for 30 days: site traffic, signups without a table, social media activity, feature counts, and document output.

## The one-line mandate

For the next 30 days, the founder's job is to seat four real people at a weekly mahjong table in Las Vegas, five times over, and to hand as many of those tables as possible to someone else to run. Nothing else gets built or written until that is done.
