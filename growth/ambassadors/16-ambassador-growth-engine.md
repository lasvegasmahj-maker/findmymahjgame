# Ambassador Growth Engine

How we reward Founding Ambassadors so the reward follows real activation, not a signup. The currency we pay for is the North Star: tables formed, filled, played, and above all recurring.

## The core principle

Pay for outcomes, not enthusiasm. A signature, a kickoff call, and a badge cost us nothing and feel good, so those can come at the start. Anything with real cost or scarcity (money, features, referrals, public spotlight) is earned by forming and filling real tables. This protects the budget, attracts doers instead of perk-seekers, and keeps the program honest.

## A blunt look at the proposed benefits

The proposed list is good but uneven. Sorted by what to actually do:

Give at signup (zero cost, high meaning):
- Founding Ambassador status and badge. Free, motivating, and time-bound (only the first cohort per city). Keep it.
- Direct founder access and a kickoff call. Free and the single best activation lever. Keep, but cap it; it does not scale past the founding cohort.
- Community launch support (templates, scripts, the launch kit). Already built. Keep.

Earn by activation (deliver only after a real table):
- Featured city profile and featured teacher listing. These are powerful, but they depend on the city pages and directory, which are not live yet. Promising them now writes a check we cannot cash. Hold until those ship, then award them when an ambassador has at least one filled table.
- Priority referrals (we send nearby players to their tables). This is the most valuable benefit we can give and it costs us nothing but routing. Make it the headline reward, earned once they have an active, filling table. It also creates a flywheel: producers get more players, so they produce more.
- Early access and product input. Cheap and flattering. Fine to give the founding cohort, but it is weak as a performance reward because it is not visible to their community.

Be careful with launch grants (see below).

## Launch grants, evaluated

Cash is the riskiest reward. It attracts the wrong people, is hard to administer, and does not scale to a national volunteer network. Recommendation by item:

- $100 launch grant: only as a reimbursement, after the first filled table, against receipts, capped, and only for real launch costs (a room booking, a set of cards, refreshments for a first open play). Not cash for signing up.
- $250 launch grant: reserve for a proven ambassador (for example, after a recurring game is running, or after 5 filled tables) who is hosting a larger first event. Discretionary, not automatic.
- Venue sponsorship: better than cash because it directly removes a real barrier and the money goes to a public place (a library room, a community center fee). Fund this for proof and launch cities, post-activation.
- Refreshments budget: small, cheap, and high-warmth for a first open play. Cap it (for example $25 to $40) and pay it after the session happens.
- Marketing credits: skip for now. We have no paid ad product to credit, so this is vague. If it means us running a local boost on their behalf, treat it as a priority-referrals benefit instead, which is cleaner and cheaper.

Net: replace broad cash grants with post-activation reimbursements (capped) plus venue sponsorship in priority cities. Spend the saved money and energy on priority referrals, which scale and compound.

## The recommended structure

Three tiers, each unlocked by real outcomes.

Tier 1, Founding Ambassador (at signup):
- Founding Ambassador status and badge, kickoff call, launch kit, referral code, early access and product input.
- Cost: near zero. Purpose: commitment and equipping.

Tier 2, Activated (first filled table that played):
- Priority referrals turned on (we route nearby players to them).
- Eligible for venue sponsorship and a refreshments reimbursement for a first open play.
- Featured city profile and teacher listing once those pages ship.
- Purpose: reward the behavior that matters and fuel more of it.

Tier 3, Anchor (a recurring game running, then scaling):
- Larger discretionary support for events ($250-style), first pick of new features, a public ambassador spotlight, and a standing place on the founder advisory group.
- Purpose: keep the strongest producers producing and make them the proof we recruit with.

## Performance milestones (what they unlock)

Tie milestones to real outcomes, and weight filled and recurring far above merely formed (a "formed" table is gameable; a recurring game that played is not).

- First table formed: encouragement note and a public welcome to the cohort. No spend.
- First table filled: priority referrals on; refreshments reimbursement unlocked. This is the real activation line.
- First game played: confirmed via the "Did you play" step; ambassador moves to Activated; eligible for venue sponsorship.
- First recurring table: the prize milestone. Anchor status, spotlight, discretionary event support.
- 5 tables: should mean 5 filled, not 5 created. Featured profile boost and a larger referral allocation.
- 10 tables: Anchor tier, advisory group, first pick of new features.
- 25 tables: city captain status, a real say in how their region grows, and our public, named gratitude.

Guardrail: count a table toward a milestone only when it reaches 4 of 4 and is confirmed played. Otherwise we will reward activity that never became a game.

## How this serves the metrics

Every reward here is gated on Activated Ambassadors, Tables Filled, Games Played, and Recurring Tables. Nothing pays for traffic, signups, or social posts. The most scalable reward (priority referrals) also directly increases Tables Filled, so the engine compounds: producers earn more players, which produces more recurring games.
