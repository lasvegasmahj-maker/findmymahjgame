# Warm Relationship Activation Plan

A focused 30-day plan to convert warm relationships into activated ambassadors who form recurring tables. Track everyone in the Warm Relationship Pipeline dashboard (/admin/pipeline). Start with Oh My Mahjong contacts and conference relationships, then teachers, organizers, studio owners, and Facebook group admins.

## Build the three lists first

Sort every warm relationship into one bucket. Spend founder time top-down.

Hot list (contact first, this week):
- Real rapport plus the ability to convene players now, and ideally in or near Las Vegas or the two launch cities.
- Examples: an Oh My Mahjong teacher you clicked with who has students; an existing host already running a game; a club organizer who liked the idea.
- These are your first ambassadors. Goal: sign and start a table on the call.

Warm list (contact within two weeks):
- A genuine but lighter connection, good profile, interest unconfirmed.
- Examples: friendly conference contacts, teachers you met briefly, studio owners open to ideas.
- Nurture with the sequence, then a call on any reply.

Cold list (low effort, keep on file):
- A name and a card, weak connection or unclear fit.
- One warm note, then leave. Do not spend founder hours here.

Re-sort as you learn. A Warm contact who replies eagerly becomes Hot.

## Who to contact first

Order, strictly:
1. Hot contacts in Las Vegas (they can produce a recurring table this month).
2. Hot contacts in Scottsdale/Phoenix and Boca/Palm Beach (the launch markets).
3. Existing hosts and organizers anywhere warm (fastest to an Organic table).
4. Hot contacts in other Top 25 cities.
5. Warm contacts in priority cities.
6. Everyone else.

## Exact outreach sequence

For Hot contacts (personal, fast):
- Touch 1 (call or text, Day 0): "Hi {{first}}, it is Shauna from Oh My Mahjong. I am starting a small group of Founding Ambassadors who help people in their city find a regular mahjong game. You were the first person I thought of for {{city}}. It is free and I will do the setup with you. Could we talk for 15 minutes this week?"
- Touch 2 (Day 2, if no reply): a short text nudge.
- Touch 3 (the call): start their first table live, give the referral code, book the follow-up.
- Then: weekly check-in until they have a filled, recurring table.

For Warm contacts (email sequence, then call on reply):
- Day 0: warm opener referencing the conference and one specific thing about them.
- Day 3: how it helps their students or members, with the launch kit.
- Day 7: offer a 15-minute call to start a table together.
- Day 12: friendly final note, then move to monthly check-in.

For Cold contacts:
- One note referencing the conference. One optional follow-up in a month. Then leave.

## Weekly targets (Las Vegas-first, 30 days)

These are founder activity targets that drive the funnel. Adjust to your real list size.

- Week 1: contact all Hot Las Vegas contacts; sign 2 to 3 ambassadors; start 2 to 3 tables.
- Week 2: contact Hot launch-city contacts and existing hosts; sign 2 more ambassadors; fill the Week 1 tables.
- Week 3: work the Warm list in priority cities; first recurring games confirmed; sign 1 to 2 more.
- Week 4: convert; aim for at least 5 activated ambassadors total and 5 recurring Las Vegas tables.

Pipeline movement target each week: move at least 5 contacts forward one stage (Identified to Contacted to Conversation to Candidate to Approved to Activated). No Approved contact should sit more than 7 days without a nudge.

## Ambassador conversion targets

Funnel math to aim for over 30 days:
- Identify and enter: 30 to 50 warm contacts in the pipeline.
- Contact: at least 20 personally.
- Conversations: 12 to 15 real conversations.
- Candidates: 8 to 10 who want to help.
- Approved: 6 to 8 ambassadors.
- Activated (started a real table): at least 5.

The only target that counts is Activated, and behind it, recurring tables. Signed-but-not-activated is not progress. The biggest leak is the gap between Approved and Activated, so guard it: every approved ambassador gets a founder-assisted first table within two weeks.

## Where founder time goes

- Most of it: Hot Las Vegas calls that sign an ambassador and start a table.
- A strong slice: activating already-approved ambassadors (first filled table). Higher value than finding new names.
- A little: Warm nurture in priority cities.
- Almost none: Cold contacts and unplanned cities.
- The test for any founder hour: does it move a contact one stage toward Activated, or a table toward filled and recurring. If not, do not spend it.
