# Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly

A weekly email from Find My Mahj Game. Warm, plain, senior-friendly, and built so every reader can DO something this week.

---

## Part 1. Layout and Standing Sections (the repeatable template)

Use this same skeleton for every issue. Keep the whole thing to about one phone screen. Big text, short lines, one main button.

**FROM:** Shauna at Find My Mahj Game
**REPLY-TO:** hello@findmymahjgame.com
**SUBJECT:** (see each issue)
**PREHEADER:** One friendly line that previews the issue (the gray text after the subject in the inbox).

**Standing sections, always in this order:**

1. **Header.** "Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly" and the date line: "{{day}}, {{city}} edition."
2. **A warm hello (2-3 sentences).** From Shauna. Personal, plain, no hype.
3. **Tables needing a 4th this week.** 1-3 listings. Each shows neighborhood, day, time, public venue, level, and a CLAIM SEAT button to /t/CODE. First names only. No addresses, no phone numbers.
4. **New tables forming by neighborhood.** Where a group is starting and needs players. Points to /start (to start one) or /play (to join one).
5. **Open plays and events this week.** Drop-in games, classes, and meetups at public venues. Day, time, place, level.
6. **Learn a little: tip or teacher spotlight.** One short beginner tip OR a short instructor spotlight.
7. **A community moment.** One real, human note: a milestone, a thank-you, a photo caption, a first win.
8. **This week's one thing (the main call to action).** A single, clear ask with one button.
9. **Footer.** Safety line, how to get listed, how to reply, unsubscribe.

**The calls to action (use these everywhere, always as a button or a plain link):**

- **Start a table** → findmymahjgame.com/start
- **Find a game** → findmymahjgame.com/play
- **Claim a seat at a specific table** → findmymahjgame.com/t/CODE
- **Become a Founding Ambassador** → findmymahjgame.com/ambassadors
- **Just reply** → reply to the email with your neighborhood, or write hello@findmymahjgame.com

**Standing footer text (paste at the bottom of every issue):**

> **Safety first.** New groups meet in public places like libraries, community centers, senior centers, and cafes. We never share home addresses or personal phone numbers, and we use first names only in public.
> **Want your game listed?** Reply with your neighborhood, day, and time, or start one at findmymahjgame.com/start.
> You are on this list because you are part of the Las Vegas mahjong community. Reply STOP to stop, or click unsubscribe below.
> Find My Mahj Game, Las Vegas. hello@findmymahjgame.com

## Part 3. Signup Strategy (grow the list from the existing audience and from the product)

**Principle:** the fastest signup is a reply. For seniors, "reply with your neighborhood" beats any form. Capture, then add to the list and the Airtable People table.

**From the existing audience (warm, do this now):**

1. **The launch email itself is a signup engine.** Every reply with a neighborhood is a confirmed, engaged subscriber. Tag them by neighborhood in Airtable so future issues can be lightly personalized.
2. **Forward-to-a-friend ask.** Add one line near the bottom of every issue: "Know someone who would love this? Forward it. They can join by replying with their neighborhood." Friend referrals are the best source for this age group.
3. **Ambassadors collect signups in person.** Give each ambassador a half-page paper sign-up sheet (first name, neighborhood, email) for classes and open plays. Shauna enters them into Airtable and the list weekly. Paper first, because it works at a senior center table.
4. **At every class and open play, one sentence.** "We send a free weekly note with open seats and new tables near you. Want in? Print your name and email here." A clipboard on the table converts.
5. **Mine the Airtable CRM.** Everyone in People, Attendance, and past Sessions who is not yet on the weekly list gets a short personal invite. Corporate Leads and Organizations get a tailored note.

**From the product (every surface should capture an email):**

1. **/start and /play:** when someone starts or looks for a table, offer the weekly: "Get a weekly note with open seats near you." One checkbox or one field.
2. **/t/CODE claim flow:** anyone who claims a seat is added to the weekly by default with a clear note, since they are clearly active.
3. **/ambassadors:** ambassador applicants join automatically; they are your core.
4. **Confirmation moments:** after any signup or seat claim, the confirmation screen and email both say "You are now getting Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly," with a simple way to opt out.
5. **Neighborhood as the magic word:** every capture point asks for neighborhood. It powers relevance and makes each issue feel local.

**Hygiene:** honor STOP and unsubscribe instantly, never publish emails, keep first names only in anything public, and de-duplicate against Airtable weekly.

## Part 4. Distribution Plan (channels, cadence, who sends, how ambassadors amplify)

**Cadence:**
- **Weekly email,** same day and time every week (consistency builds the habit). Pick one weekday morning and hold it.
- **Content cutoff** two days before send so seat listings are accurate.

**Who sends:**
- **Shauna sends** from the Find My Mahj Game email platform, reply-to hello@findmymahjgame.com. One sender, one warm voice, for now. Manual-first.
- **Production rhythm:** Shauna (or an ambassador helper) gathers the week's open seats and forming tables from Airtable, drops them into the template, sends. Thirty minutes, not three hours.

**Channels (in priority order):**
1. **Email to the Las Vegas Mahjong list.** Primary channel. This is the asset.
2. **Ambassadors forward and read aloud.** Each ambassador forwards the issue to their own neighborhood contacts and reads the "open seats" section aloud at their weekly table and open play.
3. **Printed one-pager at venues.** Print the "open seats and events this week" section as a single page for library and senior center bulletin boards and the welcome table. Include "Join the weekly: reply with your neighborhood to hello@findmymahjgame.com" and the /play link.
4. **Warm Oh My Mahjong relationships.** Share issues with teachers, organizers, and studio owners from those relationships so they can pass them to their Las Vegas students. Co-promote their open plays in the events section.
5. **Existing platform.** Link the current week's issue from findmymahjgame.com so visitors can subscribe and see we are active.

**How ambassadors amplify (give them a simple weekly job):**
- **Forward** the issue to their personal list with one sentence of their own.
- **Read the open seats aloud** at their game and help a newcomer claim one.
- **Post the printed page** at their local library or senior center.
- **Collect signups** on the paper sheet and send Shauna the names.
- **Send Shauna one open seat or one forming table** from their neighborhood each week, so the next issue always has fresh, real, local content.

**Measure what matters (manual is fine):** seats claimed per issue, replies per issue, new signups per issue, new tables formed, and ambassadors recruited. If a section is not driving action, cut it. Speed over perfection.
