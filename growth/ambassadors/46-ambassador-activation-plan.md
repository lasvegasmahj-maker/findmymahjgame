# Ambassador Activation Plan
## Find My Mahj Game | Founding Ambassador Program
### Las Vegas, the First Market

This is the playbook for turning a signed Founding Ambassador into a producing one. Recruitment is handled elsewhere. This document is only about getting real things to happen fast: a table that fills, an event on the map, a story in the newsletter, and a new person, teacher, or venue brought into the fold.

---

## What "Activated" Means (the only definition that counts)

An ambassador is **Activated** when they have completed all four first milestones inside their first 30 days:

1. **First table:** started one table and filled at least 3 of the other seats (4 players total, or 4 plus a sub).
2. **First event:** listed or hosted one open play or event at a public venue, live on the platform.
3. **First newsletter contribution:** sent one usable item (a local note, a photo, or a short story) that we can run in Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly.
4. **First referral:** brought one new person into the pipeline (a player, a teacher, or a venue) who took a real step (claimed a seat, replied, or agreed to a conversation).

Two lighter status labels lead up to it:

- **Signed:** agreed to be a Founding Ambassador. Not yet doing anything.
- **Warming:** completed at least one milestone, working on the rest.
- **Activated:** all four milestones complete. This is the goal.
- **Anchor:** Activated, plus their first table has met a second time. This is the real win, because a table that meets twice is a habit, not an event.

The number that matters for the next 30 days is **Activated ambassadors**, with **Anchors** as the deeper signal underneath it.

---

## The Order to Pursue the Milestones

Do not let an ambassador chase all four at once. The order is deliberate, because each milestone makes the next one easier and warmer.

| Order | Milestone | Why it goes here |
|-------|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | **First table** | It is the thing they care about most and the thing they can do with people they already know. A filled table creates the photo, the story, and the natural referrals. Everything else flows from it. |
| 2 | **First event** | Once they have run one table, listing an open play is a small step up, and it feeds the inventory blitz that makes us the source of truth. |
| 3 | **First newsletter contribution** | By now they have a real table and a real event, so they have something true to share. The contribution is a byproduct, not a chore. |
| 4 | **First referral** | The referral is strongest last, because now they are inviting someone into something that visibly exists, not a promise. |

Rule of thumb: **table, then event, then story, then referral.** If an ambassador is energized and wants to jump ahead, let them, but never push milestones 2 through 4 before there is a table.

## Milestone 1: First Table

**The play.** The ambassador starts one table and fills the open seats from people they already know: a regular foursome, a couple of friends from a class, neighbors. We give them the table page so a real seat exists to claim. They pick a public venue and a recurring day and time. They aim for 4 players, or 4 plus a sub so a no-show never kills the game.

**Concrete steps for the ambassador:**
1. Start the table at findmymahjgame.com/start (we can do this for them on a 10-minute call and hand them the link).
2. Pick a public spot: a library meeting room, a community center, a senior center, or a cafe with table space in their part of town (Summerlin, Henderson, Green Valley, Anthem, Sun City, Centennial Hills, Spring Valley, or central Las Vegas).
3. Set a regular {{day}} and {{time}}.
4. Send the table link to 6-8 people so 3 seats fill fast.

**Ready-to-send nudge (text or email to the ambassador):**

> Subject: Your first table is one message away
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Let's get your first table going this week. I have already set up your table page, so there is a real seat for people to claim: {{table_link}}
>
> Here is all you do:
> 1. Pick a public spot in {{city}} (a library, community center, senior center, or a cafe with table space). I can suggest a few if you tell me your neighborhood.
> 2. Pick a regular day and time, like every {{day}} at {{time}}.
> 3. Forward your table link to 6-8 friends or classmates. You only need 3 to say yes.
>
> Reply with your neighborhood and your preferred day, and I will send you two or three specific venues to choose from.
>
> Warmly,
> Shauna

**Timeline:** Table created within 3 days of signing. Filled (3 seats claimed) within 7 days.

**If they stall:**
- **No venue:** We hand them 2-3 specific public venues in their neighborhood with the days and hours table play is welcome. Remove the research from their plate.
- **Cannot fill seats:** We pull 5-8 names from the Las Vegas Mahjong email list in their area and offer to invite them to the table directly, or we add the table to /play so nearby players find it.
- **Nervous about hosting:** We offer to co-host the first session by phone or in person, or pair them with an already-Activated ambassador nearby as a buddy.

## Milestone 2: First Event

**The play.** The ambassador lists or hosts one open play or event at a public venue and puts it live on the platform so anyone can find it. This is the milestone that feeds the inventory blitz: every listed event is a piece of the map that makes us the source of truth in Las Vegas.

**Concrete steps for the ambassador:**
1. Turn their existing table day into an "open play" once a month, where any nearby player can drop in, or list a one-time teach-and-play afternoon.
2. List it on the platform so it appears in /play and on the local map.
3. Set a public venue, a date, a time, and a simple "all levels welcome" or "beginners welcome" note.

**Ready-to-send nudge:**

> Subject: Let's put your group on the map
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Your table is rolling, so here is the next easy win. Let's list one open play in {{city}} that any nearby player can join. This is how new people in your area find you, and how we build the most complete map of mahjong in Las Vegas.
>
> The simplest version: once a month, open one of your regular {{day}} sessions to drop-ins and mark it "all levels welcome." I will create the listing for you. Just tell me the date, the venue, and whether beginners are welcome.
>
> Reply with those three things and I will have it live the same day.
>
> Warmly,
> Shauna

**Timeline:** Event listed within 14 days of signing (within 7 days of the first table filling).

**If they stall:**
- **Worried no one will come:** Frame it as turning one existing session into an open one. No new commitment, just an open door. We seed the first open play with 2-3 names from the email list.
- **Venue cannot host a bigger group:** We help them find a public venue with a free meeting room (most branch libraries and senior centers in the valley have one) and provide the list.
- **Does not know what to list:** We offer two ready templates, a monthly open play and a one-time beginners' teach-and-play, and they pick one. We write the listing.

## Milestone 3: First Newsletter Contribution

**The play.** The ambassador sends one usable item for Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly. It can be tiny: a photo of their table, a sentence about a player who just learned to recognize that the Red dragon is the Crak, a note that the Henderson library has a great quiet room, or a heads-up that a new card season is coming this spring. We do the writing and polishing; they supply the truth.

**Concrete steps for the ambassador:**
1. Send one of: a photo (with everyone's okay, first names only), a short local tip, or a one-paragraph story from their table.
2. That's it. We edit and run it.

**Ready-to-send nudge:**

> Subject: One photo or one sentence for the newsletter
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly goes out to players all over the valley, and I would love to feature your group. It takes you one minute.
>
> Send me any one of these:
> - A photo of your table (just make sure everyone is okay with it, and we use first names only).
> - One sentence about a fun moment, like a beginner catching their first Joker.
> - A local tip, like a venue with a great room or a quiet afternoon that works well for play.
>
> I will write it up and send you the final version before it runs. Just reply with whatever you have.
>
> Warmly,
> Shauna

**Timeline:** Contribution received within 21 days of signing.

**If they stall:**
- **"I have nothing to share":** We ask three specific yes/no questions ("Did anyone new join? Did anyone have a funny hand? What's the room like?") and build the item from their one-line answers.
- **Camera-shy table:** A photo of the tiles and the table, no faces, is plenty. We make that the default option.
- **Too busy:** We offer to do a 5-minute phone call and write the item from what they tell us.

## Milestone 4: First Referral

**The play.** The ambassador brings one new person into the Warm Relationship Pipeline: a player who claims a seat, a teacher who agrees to a conversation, or a venue that agrees to host play. The referral is strongest now because the ambassador is inviting someone into something real and visible, not a pitch.

**Concrete steps for the ambassador:**
1. Think of one person: a friend who wants to learn, a teacher they met (an Oh My Mahjong connection counts), or a venue manager who likes the idea of a regular game.
2. Make a warm introduction, or simply send them the right link: /play to find a game, /start to start one, or hello@findmymahjgame.com to reach us.
3. Tell us who they sent so we follow up and they get credit.

**Ready-to-send nudge:**

> Subject: Who is the one person you'd bring in?
>
> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> You have a table, an event, and you're in the newsletter. Here is the last first step, and it's the one that grows everything.
>
> Think of one person who should be part of this:
> - A friend who has wanted to learn or get back to the table (send them findmymahjgame.com/play).
> - A teacher you know or met at a conference (I would love an introduction).
> - A venue that would welcome a regular game (a cafe, a community center, a senior center).
>
> Just reply with their first name and how to reach them, or forward them my way at hello@findmymahjgame.com, and I will take it from there. You get the credit, I do the work.
>
> Warmly,
> Shauna

**Timeline:** Referral made within 30 days of signing. This is the milestone that completes activation.

**If they stall:**
- **Can't think of anyone:** We prompt with categories ("a neighbor, a former classmate, your instructor, a cafe you like") rather than asking for a name cold.
- **Shy about asking:** We make it a hand-off, not an ask. They give us the name; we make the approach. They never have to sell.
- **Wants to refer a venue but unsure how:** We give them a one-line script ("Would you be open to hosting a friendly mahjong game once a week?") and we handle everything after a yes.

## The Weekly Founder Routine (how Shauna drives activation)

Activation does not happen on its own. It happens because someone runs the same loop every week. Block 60-90 minutes, same day each week, ideally Monday so the week's nudges go out early.

**Monday: The Activation Hour**

1. **Open the board (10 min).** Pull up the ambassador tracker (see the table below). Read every ambassador's status: Signed, Warming, Activated, Anchor. Note who moved and who didn't since last week.

2. **Triage by milestone (10 min).** Sort ambassadors into three buckets:
   - **Moving:** completed a milestone in the last 7 days. Send a quick congratulations and the nudge for their next milestone.
   - **Stuck:** no movement in 7 days. Identify which milestone they're stuck on and apply the matching recovery move.
   - **Cold:** no movement in 14 days. These get a personal call this week, not another email.

3. **Send the nudges (20 min).** Each ambassador gets exactly one nudge for their current milestone, personalized with their name, city, and table link. One ambassador, one ask. Never send two milestones at once.

4. **Clear the hand-offs (15 min).** Do the work you promised: create table pages, write event listings, pull 5-8 names from the email list for tables that need players, draft newsletter items from the week's photos and notes, follow up on referred names.

5. **Update the board and the CRM (10 min).** Log status changes in the tracker and in the Airtable CRM (People for new referrals, Sessions for new tables and events, Attendance for fills, Organizations for new venues, Instructors for referred teachers). Move referred people into the Warm Relationship Pipeline.

6. **Pick this week's call (5 min).** Choose the one Cold ambassador to call personally this week. A real conversation un-sticks more than any email.

**Throughout the week:** When a milestone completes, send the congratulations and the next nudge the same day. Momentum is the whole game.

## Failure and Recovery: the General Moves

Milestone-specific recovery is above. These are the patterns that apply to any stall.

**The 7-day rule.** No movement in 7 days triggers a nudge tailored to the exact milestone they're stuck on. Not a generic check-in. A specific, small, do-it-for-them offer.

**The 14-day rule.** No movement in 14 days triggers a personal phone call, not another email. Most stalls are not unwillingness; they are uncertainty about the next small step or simply being busy. A two-minute call fixes more than a week of emails.

**Always shrink the ask.** Every recovery move makes the next step smaller, not louder. "Just reply with your neighborhood." "Just send one photo." "Just give me the first name." The ambassador should never face a blank page or a research task. We do the heavy lifting; they supply the local truth and the relationships.

**Do it for them.** The default recovery is to take the task off their plate: we create the table page, we write the listing, we draft the newsletter item, we make the referral approach. They approve; we execute.

**The buddy move.** A stalled ambassador near an Activated one gets paired up. People do for a peer what they will not do for a form. A 15-minute "here's how I filled my table" call between two ambassadors un-sticks more than any nudge.

**Re-anchor on the why.** If an ambassador has gone cold, the call is not about milestones. It is about the mission: you are helping people in {{city}} find their game and their people. Reconnect them to that, and the small steps follow.

## Tie-In: The Warm Relationship Pipeline

Activation is the engine that feeds the Warm Relationship Pipeline. Each milestone pours people and assets into it.

| Milestone | What it adds to the pipeline | Where it lands |
|-----------|------------------------------|----------------|
| **First table** | New players (the seat-claimers), a recurring Session | People + Sessions + Attendance in the CRM |
| **First event** | Inventory on the map, new drop-in players | Sessions + new People; feeds the inventory blitz |
| **First newsletter contribution** | Content that warms the whole list and recruits more tables | Las Vegas Mahjong Weekly + the email list |
| **First referral** | A new player, teacher, or venue, warmly introduced | People, Instructors, or Organizations + Corporate Leads in the CRM |

The flow: warm relationships (the email list, Oh My Mahjong contacts, existing players) are where ambassadors are sourced. Activated ambassadors then **refill** the pipeline with new players, teachers, and venues, who become the next tables, the next events, and the next ambassadors. The pipeline and activation are one loop, not two stages.

**The handshake to watch:** every referred teacher and venue from Milestone 4 should land in the CRM (Instructors, Organizations, or Corporate Leads) and re-enter the warm pipeline within the same week. That is how activation compounds: each Activated ambassador makes the next one easier to find and faster to start.
