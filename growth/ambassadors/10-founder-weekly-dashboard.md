## Where each number comes from

- Ambassador Applications: admin dashboard, Ambassadors tab (count of all).
- Ambassadors Approved: Ambassadors tab (status Approved or Contacted).
- Activated Ambassadors: ambassador tracking sheet (anyone with a first table). Cross-check against the metrics dashboard.
- Cities Active: tracking sheet (distinct cities with at least one table).
- Tables Created, Tables Filled, Games Played, Recurring Tables: /admin/metrics (the North Star funnel).

If the tracking sheet and /admin/metrics disagree, the product data wins.

## The 15-minute Friday ritual

1. Open /admin/metrics and the tracking sheet.
2. Fill the This Week table above. Mark each row On track, Ahead, or Behind.
3. Pick the single most important fix for any Behind row (usually: get a signed ambassador to start their first table, or push the launch-kit invite scripts so tables fill).
4. Note this week's top 3 ambassadors to thank.
