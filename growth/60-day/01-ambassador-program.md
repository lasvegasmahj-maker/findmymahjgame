# THE FOUNDING CITY AMBASSADOR PROGRAM

A self-contained operating plan. Everything below is written to be copy-pasted, sent, or built next week. Note on the build: there is no user-auth or ambassador table in Supabase today, and the owner is non-technical and low-ops. So this program is designed to run for the first 60 days with zero new login system: a Tally/Typeform application, a Google Sheet roster, the existing `inquiries` table, and one new lightweight `ambassadors` table only when we are ready to render badges. Where code is needed it is minimal and called out explicitly.

## 1. PROGRAM CONCEPT, AND WHY TEACHER-FIRST WINS

**Concept in one line:** Recruit one founding ambassador per city who "owns" that city on Find My Mahj, gets a public badge and status, and in exchange seeds the first 5+ real listings (the Minimum Viable Liquidity bar) and keeps the city alive.

**The job FindMyMahj actually has.** A directory is worthless empty and unstoppable once full. The hard part is the cold start in each of hundreds of cities. We cannot personally fill 300 cities. We need one motivated human per city who already has a reason to want that city full. That person is a mahjong teacher.

**Why teacher-first beats player-first, venue-first, or DIY:**

1. **Instant liquidity, day one.** A teacher arrives with 8-40 current and former students. That is 5+ listings (our MVL bar) the same week they say yes, not a 6-week recruiting grind. Players arrive with 3 friends; teachers arrive with a roster.
2. **Recurring supply, not a one-time dump.** Teachers run new classes every season. Every new beginner class is a new cohort of people who finish lessons and immediately need a 4th. The teacher becomes a renewable listing source, perfectly timed to the spring NMJL card cycle.
3. **Authority and trust transfer.** A certified or active instructor is the local mahjong authority. When she says "list yourself on Find My Mahj," students do it. A stranger's cold email does not convert; their own teacher's recommendation does. This is the same credibility lever Shauna uses on lasvegasmahj.com.
4. **They are already findable and motivated.** Teachers market themselves publicly (Instagram, Facebook groups, LibCal/rec-center class pages, Oh My Mahjong's instructor network). They have a standing business reason to want "where to play near me" solved, because "I took lessons, now what?" is the #1 question they field and cannot scale answering.
5. **Aligned incentive, no payment required.** A full local directory makes the teacher look like the hub of her city's mahjong scene and feeds her future classes. Status and reach are the currency she already chases. That is exactly the lever we have (revenue is not).
6. **Owner-to-ambassador peer credibility.** Shauna is a certified NMJL instructor. Teacher-to-teacher outreach from a peer who built a real site converts far better than "marketer wants you to promote our app."

**The competitive logic:** Whoever locks the teacher in each city first owns that city's mahjong graph. Teachers are a finite, identifiable set. This is a land grab. Sign them before anyone realizes the asset exists.

**Fallback ladder (when no teacher exists or says yes in a city):** active league/club organizer -> rec-center or JCC/library mahjong program coordinator -> a "super-connector" enthusiast who runs the local Facebook group. Same program, same badge, teacher just gets first right of refusal.

## 2. AMBASSADOR RESPONSIBILITIES, AND WHAT "GOOD" LOOKS LIKE IN MONTH 1

**The deal, plain:** You become the Founding Ambassador for your city. You help your city's page cross 5 real listings and keep it healthy. We give you a public badge, founder status, tools, and co-marketing with Shauna. Time commitment: about 1-2 hours in week one, then 20-30 minutes a month.

**Core responsibilities:**

1. **Seed the city (the one that matters).** Get at least 5 real player listings live on your city via the free `/list-my-game` flow. Easiest path: forward your students one link and one sentence.
2. **Be the local face.** Your name/handle and badge appear on your city/state page as Founding Ambassador. You are the human who makes the page feel real.
3. **Keep it fresh.** Add or flag new open-play sessions, classes, and club meetups as you hear about them. Tell us when something dies so we never show stale info (honors our data-honesty rule).
4. **Welcome new players.** When someone new lists in your city, you are the friendly first contact who tells them where to actually play.
5. **One amplification.** Share your city page once to your audience (post, story, or newsletter). We supply the graphic and caption.

**What "good" looks like at end of Month 1 (the activation scorecard):**

| Metric | Floor (activated) | Good | Founding-tier great |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real player listings seeded in city | 5 | 8-12 | 15+ |
| Open-play / class / club entries contributed | 1 | 3 | 5+ |
| Public share of city page | 1 | 1 + reshare by audience | drives 3+ listings from the share |
| Stale items flagged | as found | proactive monthly check | keeps page 100% accurate |
| Response time to a new lister in their city | within a week | within 48h | same day, warm welcome |

An ambassador who hits the floor (5 listings + 1 entry + 1 share) is "activated" and earns the permanent Founding badge. Below 5 listings after 30 days, they keep "Ambassador" but not "Founding," and we re-recruit a co-ambassador rather than pull the badge (never punish a volunteer publicly).

## 3. RECRUITMENT PROCESS, STEP BY STEP (WITH TARGET COUNTS)

**60-day target:** 30 activated founding ambassadors across the 30 highest-potential metros. Funnel math at realistic conversion: to land 30 activated, we need ~40 verbal yeses, ~80 calls/DMs booked, ~250 personalized first-touches, from a sourcing list of ~400 named teachers. This is very doable because teachers are public and finite.

### Step 0 - Pick the cities (Day 1)
Rank by: existing seed-data interest, lasvegasmahj audience overlap, density of public mahjong classes, and Oh My Mahjong instructor presence. Start with the top 30 metros. Las Vegas is ambassador zero (Shauna herself, as the proof-of-concept city).

### Step 1 - Build the sourcing list (Days 1-4). Target: 400 named teachers, 8-15 candidates/city.
Sources, in priority order:
1. **Oh My Mahjong instructor network** (existing partnership; ask for a city-tagged list, this is the fastest channel).
2. **Mah Jongg That! / Mahjong League / other certification directories** (public instructor finders).
3. **Instagram/TikTok**: search `mahjong lessons [city]`, `mahjong teacher [city]`, `learn mahjong [city]`; save handle, follower count, email/DM-open status.
4. **Facebook**: city mahjong groups -> identify admins and the people who post "lessons available."
5. **LibCal / rec center / JCC / senior center / country club class pages**: the named instructor on a public mahjong class.
6. **Local press**: "mahjong class" + city news hits often name the teacher.

Capture in a Google Sheet (the CRM): `City | Name | Role(teacher/league/coord) | Source | IG | FB | Email | Phone | Best channel | First touch date | Status | Notes`. Status pipeline: `Sourced -> Contacted -> Replied -> Call booked -> Verbal yes -> Application in -> Activated -> Founding`.

### Step 2 - First contact (Days 3-20). Target: 250 personalized touches, ~25% reply.
Channel by what we have: email if we have it (template 4A), else Instagram/Facebook DM (template 4C). Always personalize the first line with something real (their class, their group, their city). 8-12 sends per city. Two-business-day later, follow-up (4B) to non-repliers. **Hard rule: every external link in any outreach is fetched and confirmed 200 before sending; never paste an unverified or invented URL.**

### Step 3 - The call (Days 5-30). Target: ~80 calls, ~50% -> verbal yes.
15-minute call or DM voice note. Script in 4D. Goal: get them excited, confirm they want their city, and get one micro-commitment (they'll email students this week). End every call by sending the application link while you're still on.

### Step 4 - Activation (Days 7-40). Target: 40 verbal yeses -> 30 activated.
1. They submit the application form (section 5) -> lands in our roster.
2. Same day: send the **Founding Ambassador Welcome Kit** (one email): their city link, the student-forward template, the share graphic, the badge preview, and "reply when your 5th listing is live."
3. They forward the student template; listings come in through the existing free `/list-my-game` flow (no new infra needed).
4. When their city hits 5 real published listings, flip them to **Founding** in the roster and turn on their badge.

### Cadence to actually hit it
~12-15 first-touches/day for 3 weeks gets to 250. Batch by city so calls feel local. One spreadsheet review every Monday: who's stuck, who's close to 5, who to re-touch.

## 6. THE BADGE SYSTEM

**Purpose:** Make the founder role visibly real, create status the ambassador wants to display, and turn cities into a leaderboard so ambassadors compete to grow their city's standing.

### Tiers (gated by real published listings in the city, not effort)

| Tier | Name | Unlock | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | **Founding Ambassador (Bronze)** | Verified as founder + 5 real listings live | You started your city. |
| 2 | **Founding Ambassador (Silver)** | 15 real listings + 3 open-play/class/club entries | Your city has a real scene. |
| 3 | **Founding Ambassador (Gold)** | 30 listings + active monthly upkeep + 1 amplification that drove signups | Your city is a hub. |
| Crown | **City Champion** | Top city in its tier nationally / first to 50 listings in its population band | Best in the country. One per band at a time. |
| Always | **Founding Member** (permanent sub-mark) | Any ambassador from the first 50 cities | Locks in "Day One" status forever, even if they later go inactive. The scarcity hook. |

### Visual treatment
- Shape: a circular seal evoking a mahjong tile face, with a small flower motif (flowers are interchangeable, so it reads universally) and a tile-rack baseline.
- Metal ramp: Bronze (#B0763C) -> Silver (#9AA3AD) -> Gold (#D4A92B) -> City Champion adds a thin crown arc and the brand magenta accent (the site's #e91e8c).
- Text plate: `FOUNDING AMBASSADOR` arced top, `[CITY], [ST]` on the bottom plate, year `EST. 2026` in the center band (the "Day One" scarcity cue).
- Delivered as: an on-site SVG component (renders crisp at any size) plus two downloadable PNGs per ambassador (square for IG, wide for FB/LinkedIn) so they self-promote.

### Where badges appear on the site
1. **City/state page header** (`/states/[state]`, city section): "Founded by [Name] · Founding Ambassador [tier seal]." This is the primary status surface and what makes a thin new page feel human and credible.
2. **Player/listing cards seeded by the ambassador's city**: a small "Founding city" ribbon corner so the whole city visibly belongs to someone.
3. **A national `/ambassadors` roster + leaderboard** (added once 10+ are live): grid of city seals sorted by tier, then by listing count. This is the competition engine.
4. **Their own channels**: the downloadable PNGs, plus an embeddable "Founding Ambassador, [City]" badge they can put in their IG bio link / class page.

### How it creates status and inter-city competition
- **Public leaderboard**: `/ambassadors` ranks cities by tier and listing count. "Austin is Gold, Dallas is still Bronze" is exactly the rivalry that makes Texas teachers move.
- **Scarcity**: one founder per city + permanent "Founding Member" mark for the first 50 cities. You can never be the founder again once it's taken. That is the entire pull.
- **Race mechanics**: "First to 50 listings in your population band becomes City Champion" creates head-to-head races between comparable metros, announced in the monthly ambassador note ("3 cities are within 5 listings of Gold this month").
- **Loss aversion done kindly**: tiers only go up in public. We never publicly demote; we quietly add a co-ambassador if a city stalls, so the badge stays aspirational, never punitive.

### Minimal data model (only when ready to render badges)
A single `ambassadors` table, RLS read-only for `status='active'`, write via admin only (mirrors the existing venue/event pattern):
`id, name, public_handle, city, state, role, tier (bronze|silver|gold|champion), founding_member bool, listings_seeded int, status (pending|active|paused), activated_at, notes`. Badge tier can start as a manual admin field (Shauna sets it from the Google Sheet) and only later auto-compute from a count of published listings in that city. No auth required; ambassadors do not log in.

## 7. THE BENEFITS PACKAGE (RANKED)

Revenue is not the lever, so every benefit pays in status, reach, tools, or early access. Ranked by what actually motivates a mahjong teacher.

1. **Public founder status (the badge).** The single strongest lever. Permanent, scarce ("one per city," "Founding Member of the first 50"), and displayed where her students and peers will see it. People volunteer hard for identity and recognition.

2. **Co-promotion with Shauna across both sites.** Shauna features the ambassador and her classes to the Find My Mahj and Las Vegas Mahj audiences: a "Meet the Ambassadors" feature, shoutouts, and a backlink/mention to her class page. For a teacher who fills classes for a living, free promotion from a credible certified instructor is concrete, high-value, and costs us nothing. (This directly drives her students -> her livelihood, without us paying her.)

3. **Inbound students, automatically.** New beginners in her city searching "learn mahjong" get pointed to local teachers. The directory becomes a lead source for her classes. This is the quiet ROI that makes the volunteer role rational.

4. **Early access and a voice in the roadmap.** First to try every new feature, and a real say in what we build (a private ambassador channel/thread). Insider status that costs nothing and creates ownership.

5. **The Ambassador Toolkit.** Done-for-her assets: the student-forward email/text, a custom city share graphic, downloadable badge PNGs, and a monthly ambassador note with tips, wins, and the leaderboard. Removes all the work, which is what makes a volunteer actually act.

6. **Leveling and the leaderboard.** Bronze -> Gold -> City Champion gives a long-term game to play and a way to out-rank rival cities. Sustained motivation past the initial sign-up.

7. **Founding cohort / peer network.** Access to the other founding teachers nationally (a group thread or quarterly call). Teachers are isolated locally; connecting them to a national peer set is a genuine, low-cost perk and seeds future collaboration.

8. **First right of refusal on future paid features.** If we ever monetize (venue/event listings already exist as paid), founders get free or discounted placement and first dibs in their city. A future-value sweetener that signals "you were here first," without making money the pitch today.

**Explicitly NOT offered (and why):** cash, revenue share, or commissions. They would attract the wrong recruits (mercenaries, not community leaders), create payment/ops overhead the owner can't sustain, and undercut the authentic "I lead mahjong in my city" identity that makes teachers say yes. The whole design assumes status and reach are the currency, because for this audience they are.
